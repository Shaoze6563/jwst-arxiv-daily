1. Hydrodynamic and photoionization analysis of a Cosmic Dawn dwarf galaxy based on a JWST-inferred star formation history

相关度 15

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, galaxies, cosmic dawn, star formation, reionization, stellar mass, stellar population, spectral energy distribution
作者：B. Melekh, S. Chudyk, I. Koshmak
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-12

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We investigate the evolution of the emergent spectrum of the Cosmic Dawn dwarf galaxy 2779 using coupled hydrodynamics and photoionization modeling. Evolutionary sequences of spherically symmetric, multi-component models track the star-formation history -- derived from JWST data -- from $z \approx 29.5$ to $9.5$. BPASS stellar population synthesis is coupled with the evolution of the superwind bubble and the surrounding nebular environment. The emergent spectra include escaping stellar radiation, nebular lines and continuum, as well as X-ray emission from the hot superwind cavity. At early stages, the nebular shell suppresses the escape of Lyman-continuum (Lyc) photons, while allowing X-ray emission to emerge. Significant Lyc escape appears near $z \approx 20$, followed by a non-monotonic evolution of the escape fraction ($f_{\text{esc}}$) and the X-ray luminosity per unit star-formation rate ($L_X/\text{SFR}$). Including diffuse ionizing radiation from the nebula can result in an effective escape fraction $f_{\text{esc}} > 1$. The model sequences terminate at a stage of stagnation or the complete displacement of the outer nebular component into a shell. A comparison with JWST photometry indicates that the final model spectrum generally reproduces the shape of the observed spectral energy distribution, although the overall flux level is too high. An illustrative comparison with a previous model having a lower stellar mass indicates that accounting for the evolving contribution of the nebula can reduce the stellar mass required to match observations. However, a more precise determination of stellar mass requires a self-consistent refinement of the star formation history. These models serve as a basis for constructing representative grids of models, as well as for conducting careful checks on whether dwarf galaxies could have driven the reionization of the Universe on their own.

2. JWST/NIRISS-SOSS Spectroscopic Variability and Auroral Modelling of Warm Ultracool Dwarf LSR J1835+3259

分类：astro-ph.SR, astro-ph.EP
首次提交：2026-11-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Auroral variability in ultracool dwarfs (UCDs) has been proposed as a key source of optical and infrared modulation, yet its physical origin remains poorly understood. We present JWST Near Infrared Imager and Slitless Spectrograph/Single Object Slitless Spectroscopy (NIRISS/SOSS) time-series spectroscopy of the M8.5 UCD LSR J1835+3259. These observations probe the object's wavelength-dependent rotational variability, providing insight into the atmospheric processes underlying its infrared behaviour. Additional GTC/MEGARA optical spectra reveal that its H$α$ emission has declined markedly since earlier epochs, suggesting a reduced activity state during the JWST observations. We detect no emission from H$_3^+$ or absorption by hydronium in the NIRISS spectra. We use principal component analysis to identify two distinct modes of spectral variability: one dominated by FeH modulation near 1 $μ$m and another contributing broadband variability across the H and K bands. We find that no model atmosphere grid provides a reasonable fit to the average NIRISS spectrum. However, a blended model comprising an M-dwarf photosphere and a cooler blackbody component provides an improved fit to the mean spectrum, the broadband spectral energy distribution and the NIRISS broadband variability. By adding perturbations to PICASO model grids, we demonstrate that a combination of atmospheric heating and non-equilibrium chemistry can successfully mimic a blend of unperturbed photosphere and a blackbody, and that the presence of these effects may explain the unusual spectrum and variability of LSR J1835+3259.

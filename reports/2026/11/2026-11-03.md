## 1. Physically Motivated Stellar Populations for Galaxies at Cosmic Dawn with a Temperature-Dependent IMF

- **相关度：** 11
- **匹配词：** James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies, galaxy, cosmic dawn, high redshift, stellar population
- **作者：** Gibson B. Bowling, Charles L. Steinhardt, Lachlan J. Hobart, Carter Meyerhoff, Jackson Headon, Christopher Cain, Timothy Carleton, Kevin S. Croker, Hunter D. Sparks, Maria J. Sanchez Rincon, Maksymilian T. Malinski, Zachary Borowiak, Caleb S. Watson, Rafael Ortiz, Rogier A. Windhorst, Rolf A. Jansen, Seth H. Cohen, Tanish Parida
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-01
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01595) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01595v1)

The first few years of James Webb Space Telescope observations have identified previously undetected galaxies at $z>12$ and provided new constraints on galaxies at $z>8$. Many of these objects have stellar masses that are difficult to reconcile with $Λ$CDM, inferred from models not calibrated for the high-redshift Universe. This necessitates new, more astrophysically plausible stellar population models for estimating stellar masses of galaxies at cosmic dawn. We introduce a simple stellar population (SSP) library, Simple Stellar Populations with Temperature (SSPT), computed using a temperature-dependent stellar initial mass function and its implementation in the public code CIGALE. When used in a configuration designed for high-redshift galaxies that requires young stellar population ages, the new models predict stellar masses that are ~0.3-0.5 dex lower than estimates from similar CIGALE analyses using models calibrated to the local Universe, and nearly 2 dex lower than estimates from analyses using other codes and softer constraints on stellar ages. Application of these physically motivated stellar populations suggests that stellar masses of early star-forming galaxies are not in tension with $Λ$CDM, and that galaxy formation from virialized halos remains a reasonable hypothesis at the highest redshifts. The SSPT SSPs and their CIGALE implementation are released publicly.

## 2. Developing Environments: Formation and Quenching at Cosmic Noon Traced by the Large-scale Distribution of Gas and Galaxies

- **相关度：** 9
- **匹配词：** JWST, NIRSpec, COSMOS, COSMOS-Web, galaxies, galaxy, star formation, AGN, quiescent, dust attenuation, protocluster
- **作者：** Amir H. Khoram, Sirio Belli, Trevor Mendel, Letizia Bugiani, Matteo Sapori, Andrew B. Newman, Hossein Hatamnia, Finn Giddings, Rebecca L. Davies, Olga Cucciati, Bianca Poggianti, Amirnezam Amiri, Anita Zanella, Tianmu Gao, Gabriel Maheson, Stefano Sotira, Caterina Liboni, Giulia Papini
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-30
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00190) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00190v1)

Environmental density correlates with galaxy properties in the local Universe, but how and when these trends emerge is unclear: at cosmic noon, large-scale structures are still assembling and galaxies quench rapidly for debated reasons. We study how the formation and quenching histories of massive galaxies at $1.7<z<3.5$ depend on environment, using two independent tracers: galaxy number density ($δ_\#$) from COSMOS-Web and intergalactic neutral hydrogen ($δ_{HI}$, cool gas) from LATIS Ly$α$ tomography. We combine 139 Blue Jay JWST/NIRSpec galaxies with 57 DAWN JWST Archive prism spectra in COSMOS, deriving stellar masses, star formation histories, dust attenuation, and Na\,{\sc d} outflow rates from joint spectro-photometric \texttt{Prospector} fits. In massive quiescent galaxies, the formation-to-quenching interval increases with density. AGN and Na\,{\sc d} outflows are confined to $δ_\#<0.5$ and elevated Ly$α$ absorption ($δ_{HI}>1$), mostly in massive quiescent galaxies showing both. About 65\% of the sample follows a correlation between the tracers; two outlier regions stand out. A galaxy-rich, gas-poor region ($δ_\#>2$, $δ_{HI}<1$) coincides with one of the most massive Hyperion protocluster cores, where the gas is plausibly fully ionized; its quiescent galaxies formed earlier and lack significant AGN activity and outflows. A galaxy-poor, gas-rich region ($δ_\#<1$, $δ_{HI}>1$), within 60 cMpc of the densest Hyperion cores, hosts rapidly quenched galaxies with a high incidence of AGN and outflows. This \emph{developing environment}, distinct from both mature protoclusters and the field, likely traces massive halos still assembling. Galaxy counts alone cannot capture the environment at cosmic noon: jointly mapping galaxies and cool gas is crucial to understand how environment regulates gas accretion, star formation, and quenching.

JWST has extended the search for accreting black holes into the high-redshift, low-mass regime. We report a low-mass black hole candidate in MARTA 3887, a clumpy, metal-poor galaxy at $z=3.7694$, combining deep JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy, HST and JWST imaging, and archival VLT/MUSE data. We detect a broad H$α$ component with $L_{\rm Hα,b}=2.35\times10^{41}$ erg s$^{-1}$ and FWHM $=1270^{+260}_{-250}$ km s$^{-1}$, with no [O III] counterpart of comparable width. Under a broad-line-region interpretation, virial calibrations give $\log(M_{\rm BH}/M_\odot)=6.52^{+0.18}_{-0.23}$ and $λ_{\rm Edd}=0.07$, or up to 0.38 for alternative calibrations. The host comprises at least three compact clumps, with $\log(M_\star/M_\odot)=8.16^{+0.09}_{-0.10}$. If stellar photoionization dominates the narrow lines, the gas is highly ionized and metal poor, with $12+\log({\rm O/H})=7.68\pm0.04$ ($\simeq0.10\,Z_\odot$). The inferred $M_{\rm BH}/M_\star=2.3\%$ lies about 2 dex above the extrapolated local relation, suggesting that black-hole growth has outpaced the assembly of the observed host. If growth began only when the first 10% of the observed stellar mass had formed ($t_{10}=92$ Myr ago), a $10^2\,M_\odot$ seed would require a lifetime-mean Eddington ratio $\simeq5.7$; earlier growth or a more massive, e.g. direct-collapse, seed would allow sub-Eddington mean accretion. MUSE reveals extended Ly$α$ emission ($\simeq6.5$-6.9 proper kpc), offset by $+64^{+10}_{-8}$ km s$^{-1}$ from systemic, with a provisional escape fraction of $\simeq0.44$. Together with a Mg II doublet ratio close to the optically thin limit and a high [O III]/[O II] ratio, this makes MARTA 3887 a laboratory to study black-hole growth, stellar assembly, and ionizing-photon escape jointly.

## 4. An Empirical Temperature Relation for Chemical Abundances in AGN

- **相关度：** 7
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxy, AGN
- **作者：** Sophia R. Flury, Oli L. Dors, Rogemar A. Riffel, Karla Z. Arellano-Córdova, Mark Armah, Guillermo F. Hägele, Mónica V. Cardaci, Igor A. Zinchenko
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02915) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.02915v1)

We present for the very first time the empirical temperature structure of the narrow line region (NLR) clouds of active galactic nuclei (AGNs) based on a sample of [N II] and [O III] auroral line measurements. The scaling of Te([N II]) with Te([O III]), expressed as the t2-t3 relation, is key to deriving accurate chemical abundances, in AGNs and other line-emitting nebulae, particularly when only one auroral line is observed. Here, we provide the first robust empirical description of t2-t3 for AGNs: t2(10^4 K)=1.285*t3(10^4 K)+0.799. We find substantial changes in 12+log(O/H) when compared to other t2-t3 calibrations when using the direct, or Te, method with only the [O III] 4363 auroral line. We find that adopting our t2-t3 most accurately recovers the fully direct method 12+log(O/H). To interpret the physical mechanisms behind the AGN t2-t3, we compare the observed distribution of [N II] 6584/5755 and [O III] 5007/4363 flux ratios to a variety of ionization model predictions. We find evidence for power law density gradients, pressure stratification, and (sometimes significant) contributions from astrophysical shocks driving much of the dispersion on these flux ratios while metal cooling dominates the primary shape of the distribution. With more accurate t2-t3 and 12+log(O/H), the properties of the interstellar medium photoionized by AGN can be better characterized and understood across a wide range of cosmic time and may bring better context for novel observations at z>6 with JWST, with implications for understanding chemical enrichment in the earliest stages of galaxy evolution.

## 5. Targeting the future of spectroscopic galaxy surveys -- A cosmological study case with the Wide-field Spectroscopic Telescope

- **相关度：** 7
- **匹配词：** COSMOS, galaxy, galaxies, emission line
- **作者：** Constantin Payerne, Antoine Rocher, Aurélien Verdier, William d&#x27;Assignies, Behnood Bandi, Sofia Contarini, Matteo Ferro, Jean-Paul Kneib, Vincenzo Mainieri, Michele Moresco, Marco Palla, Maxime Rombach, Christophe Yèche, Furkan Akbaba, Hélène Courtois, Darko Donevski, Massimo Guidi, Valentin Ivanov, Konstantinos Migkas, Ivan Minchev, Ignacio del Moral-Castro, Olcay Plevne, Mojtaba Raouf, Laurence Tresse, the WST Collaboration
- **分类：** astro-ph.CO
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02828) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.02828v1)

The next generation of Stage-V spectroscopic surveys are designed to measure hundreds of millions of galaxy and quasar spectra, extending precision large-scale-structure cosmology to redshifts and volumes well beyond the reach of current Stage-IV facilities. Realising this potential requires revisiting the target selection strategies and designing them explicitly around the depth, multiplexing, and survey time of the new instruments, rather than a direct extrapolation of existing recipes. This work develops and characterises new strategies for the Wide-field Spectroscopic Telescope (WST), a proposed 12-metre, highly multiplexed facility expected to operate in the 2040s. Using deep multi-band photometry, including COSMOS-field data from CLAUDS and HSC, we revisit low-redshift $z<1.6$ tracer selection of SDSS, BOSS/eBOSS, DESI and 4MOST-CRS -- Bright Galaxies, Luminous Red Galaxies, Emission Line Galaxies and quasars -- to photometric depth achievable in the WST era, and introduce a purely magnitude-limited sample (MagMax) exploiting three-dimensional colour-colour star-galaxy separation, and a set of Lyman-Break Galaxy dropout samples extending target selection to $z>2$. Using Fisher-matrix forecasts, we showcase the constraining power of a WST-like cosmological survey on BAO distance parameters $α_\parallel$ and $α_\perp$, the growth rate $f$, the evolving dark energy equation $w_0w_a$, the local-type primordial non-Gaussianity $f_{\rm NL}^{\rm loc}$, and the sum of the neutrino mass $Σm_ν$. The main outcome of this work is an optimisation framework that directly links instrumental capabilities and observing time to cosmological constraining power, providing a science-driven template applicable to the design of future generations of Stage-V spectroscopic surveys.

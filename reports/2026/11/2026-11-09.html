1. Jeans Dynamical Constraints on the IMF in Massive Quiescent Galaxies at $z\sim0.7$ from JWST/NIRSpec

相关度 13

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, NIRCam, galaxies, quiescent, stellar mass, morphology
作者：Martje Slob, Mariska Kriek, Chloe M. Cheng, Arjen van der Wel, Aliza G. Beverage, Rachel Bezanson, Charlie Conroy, Anna de Graaff, Elham Eftekhari, Robert Feldmann, Wout M. Goesaert, Meng Gu, Joel Leja, Brian Lorenz, Pavel E. Mancera Piña, Ignacio Martín-Navarro, Andrew B. Newman, Sedona H. Price, Alice E. Shapley, Katherine A. Suess, Jesse van de Sande, Pieter van Dokkum
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-05

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present dynamical masses for nine massive quiescent galaxies at $z\sim0.7$ from the JWST-IMFERNO program, measured from ultra-deep ($\sim32$hr), spatially resolved NIRSpec/MSA spectroscopy combined with NIRCam imaging. The most massive of these galaxies have previously been found to have a bottom-heavy initial mass function (IMF), based on measurements of dwarf-sensitive absorption features. Here, we examine whether the stellar masses inferred for these IMFs are consistent with the dynamical masses of these galaxies. We derive the dynamical masses by including Jeans Anisotropic Modelling within a forward-modelling framework that accounts for instrumental optics, source morphology, and geometric effects. We compare the resulting dynamical masses to the stellar masses for different IMF assumptions. A Milky Way IMF produces physically plausible dynamical-to-stellar mass ratios but implies central dark matter fractions ($f_{\rm DM,1R_e}=0.54\pm0.06$) that are a factor $\sim2$ higher compared to local ETGs and simulations. When uniformly applying the IMF that was measured from absorption features, the mean dark matter fraction becomes low ($f_{\rm DM,1R_e}=0.02\pm0.18$), and the stellar masses exceed the dynamical mass at $2σ$ significance for two out of seven galaxies in the sample. When we adopt the measured IMF only in the central $R_e$ and use a MW IMF in the outskirts, we find realistic dark matter fractions ($f_{\rm DM,1R_e}=0.28\pm0.12$) and consistent dynamical masses. These results support a scenario where the cores of the most massive quiescent galaxies at $z\sim0.7$ had a bottom-heavy IMF, similar to those observed in the centres of local early-type galaxies. Our results are consistent with an inside-out growth scenario, in which size and mass growth of massive galaxies proceeds after quenching through minor mergers, leaving the bottom-heavy cores mostly intact.

2. Constraining Dust Attenuation Curves in Star-Forming Galaxies at Cosmic Noon Using Multiple Emission Line Ratios

相关度 13

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, JADES, galaxies, galaxy, emission line, dust attenuation, star formation
作者：Owen Zafuta, Joseph Havens, Bren E. Backhaus, Allison Kirkpatrick, Gregory Troiani, Kurt Hamblin
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-05

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Dust attenuation remains a dominant systematic uncertainty in measurements of an emission line, and therefore galaxies properties derived from the emission lines such as star formation rates. Corrections based only on the Balmer decrement require an assumed attenuation-curve slope, even though that slope varies among galaxies. We use JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy from JADES to constrain effective dust attenuation curves for 386 star-forming galaxies at $0.5\leq z\leq7$. For each galaxy, we combine H$α$, H$β$, and one additional hydrogen recombination line and solve for the normalization $τ_V$ and slope $n$ of a power-law attenuation curve, allowing the slope to vary from galaxy to galaxy. We found slopes and normalization span a broad range even for galaxies at similar redshifts. Additionally, our method using Paschen transitions provided substantially greater constraints the attenuation-curve slope than Balmer-only combinations. When comparing to SMC-like or Calzetti-like corrections we find our method produces more consistent star formation rates with less scatter. These improved dust corrections provide an avenue to better describe star formation rates in the epoch of peak star formation and the early universe.

3. A combined PRIMER+MINERVA study of the evolution of $\mathbf{[O\mbox{III}]+Hβ}$ equivalent width from $\mathbf{z=3}$ to $\mathbf{z=8.5}$

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present an analysis of the evolution of $[O\,III]+\mathrm{H}β$ equivalent width ($W_λ$) over the redshift range $z=3$ to $z=8.5$, based on combining the JWST NIRCam PRIMER and MINERVA imaging in the UDS survey field. We find that the combined medium- and broad-band data set reduces the photometric redshift outlier rate to $η= 4.7\%$ at $m_{\rm F444W}\leq28$, a $42\%$ reduction compared with PRIMER imaging alone. We also demonstrate that the combined data set facilitates the accurate recovery of $[O\,III]+\mathrm{H}β$ equivalent widths in redshift bins centred on $z\sim3.2,\, 4.0,\, 5.0,\, 7.2,\, 8.3$; corresponding to the F210M, F250M, F300M, F410M and F460M filters. Based on a final sample of $N=1964$ galaxies with $M_{\rm UV}\leq-19.25$, we first provide new constraints on the bright end of the $z=7-8$ $[O\,III]+\mathrm{H}β$ luminosity function ($\log_{10}φ^{*} = -4.3_{-0.5}^{+0.2}$, $\log_{10}L^{*} = -43.4_{-0.2}^{+0.3}$), before proceeding to investigate the redshift evolution of $[O\,III]+\mathrm{H}β$ equivalent width. Using SED modelling based on non-parametric continuity star-formation histories, we find that the median $[O\,III]+\mathrm{H}β$ equivalent width monotonically increases with redshift, rising from $\mathrm{log}_{10}(W_λ)=2.54^{+0.01}_{-0.01}$ at $z\simeq 3$ to $\mathrm{log}_{10}(W_λ)=3.05^{+0.03}_{-0.03}$ at $z\simeq 8$. In tandem, the distribution of $\mathrm{log}_{10}(W_λ)$ is observed to become increasingly asymmetric with redshift, producing a fraction of extreme emission-line galaxies (EELGs, $W_λ>1000\,$Å) that reaches $f_{\rm EELG}=0.50\pm{0.10}$ by $z=8.3$. Our SED modelling results indicate that the physical driver of this evolution is increasingly bursty star-formation, with EELGs the result of particularly high specific star-formation rates over the 10 Myr prior to observation.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present the strong lens mass modeling result of 60 galaxy clusters targeted by Vast Exploration for Nascent, Unexplored Sources (VENUS), the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) multi-epoch Treasury program. We obtain refined mass models for the 60 clusters with the glafic software by fitting positions of $\sim 4500$ multiple images, $\sim 1150$ of which are newly identified multiple images with help of multi-band JWST images. Each cluster contains typically $\sim 50$ multiple images, thanks to the selection of target clusters based on their high lensing strengths. We validate our models with the Jackknife method. The median Einstein radius is $\sim 30''$ and the distribution of the Einstein radii extends out to $\sim 60''$ for the source redshift of 9, which confirms that their lensing strengths are indeed among the highest known to date. For the source redshift of 9, we identify MACS J0717.5+3745, MACS J0600.1$-$2008, and PLCK G287.0+32.9 as clusters with the largest Einstein radii as well as the largest areas of the high-magnification ($|μ|>10$) regions. We find that the observed Einstein radii of VENUS clusters are fully compatible with the standard cold dark matter model.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

SN 2026ahvu is a Type Ia supernova (SN) that occurred in the nearby galaxy NGC 1433. Using archival Hubble and JWST imaging from the PHANGS survey, we show that the SN lies within 0.19$''$ and 0.26$''$ of two UV-bright star clusters. This $0.19/0.26''$ separation corresponds to $8/11$ pc ($17/24$ pc) at the reported distances of $9.1$ and $18.9$ Mpc, respectively. The probability of finding an unrelated Type Ia SN this close to a cataloged star cluster by chance is only $\sim$0.4 %. Thus, SN 2026ahvu is likely the first Type Ia SN that can be associated with relatively young star clusters in a nearby galaxy. From spectral energy distribution fitting using HST+JWST (or HST-only) data, we find that the clusters have age estimates of $37_{-5}^{+3}$ ($57_{-9}^{+14}$) Myr and $95_{-13}^{+15}$ ($88_{-12}^{+14}$) Myr. Association with either cluster would mean that SN 2026ahvu should be considered a prompt SN Ia and provide one of the most concrete measurements to date of a prompt Ia delay time. The median age of the younger star cluster is close to the minimum possible delay time for SN Ia progenitors. If the SN is indeed associated with the younger cluster, it would imply that Type Ia SNe can occur almost immediately after white dwarf formation. If the SN is associated with the older cluster, this discovery still presents the first example of a prompt Type Ia SN with delay time under 100 Myr.

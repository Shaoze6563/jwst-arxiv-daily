1. AGN Host Galaxies in COSMOS-Web: Linking Structural Evolution and Star Formation Quenching up to $z=5$

相关度 15

匹配词：COSMOS, COSMOS-Web, JWST, galaxies, star formation, AGN, stellar mass, quiescent
作者：Xiao Han, Lilan Yang, T. Kelly, Matthew A. Malkan, Luis C. Ho, John D. Silverman, Zi-Jian Li, Andreas L. Faisst, Andrew J. Battisti, Jeyhan S. Kartaltepe, Lauro Moscardini, Rasha M. Samir, Ronaldo Laishram, Tao Wang, Yiyang Zhang, Caitlin M. Casey, Henry Joy McCracken, Jason Rhodes, Brant E. Robertson, Maximilien Franco, Daizhong Liu, Marko Shuntov, Xuheng Ding
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-04

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The role of active galactic nuclei (AGNs) in the suppression of star formation and structural evolution of galaxies during their transition from star-forming to quiescent systems remains unclear. We analyze 3,955 AGNs selected through five multiwavelength diagnostics at $0<z<5$. Using high-resolution JWST imaging from the COSMOS-Web survey and archival multi-wavelength photometry, we investigate the rest-frame optical size-mass distribution of AGN host galaxies and compare it with those of star-forming galaxies (SFGs) and quiescent galaxies (QGs). We find that the positions of AGN hosts in the size-mass plane depend on both redshift and stellar mass. At $z\gtrsim2$, massive AGN hosts ($M_\star/M_\odot>10^{10.5}$) generally exhibit sizes comparable to those of SFGs despite suppressed star formation. Toward lower redshifts, they increasingly approach the QG size-mass relation, accompanied by declining specific star formation rates (sSFRs) and increasing Sérsic indices. In contrast, lower-mass AGN hosts remain close to the SFG relation across the redshift range. Overall, AGN hosts exhibit relatively mild size evolution, following $R_e\propto(1+z)^{-0.6}$ over $0<z<4$, shallower than that of typical SFGs and QGs. We further examine the relationship between star formation activity and structural compactness in the $Δ\log\mathrm{SFR}_{\mathrm{MS}}$-$ΔΣ_e$ plane. At $z<2.2$, AGN hosts predominantly occupy the compact star-forming and quiescent regions, broadly consistent with the compaction-quenching scenario. At $z>2.2$, however, some AGN hosts exhibit suppressed star formation while retaining relatively extended structures. These findings suggest that some AGN hosts may follow alternative evolutionary pathways in which star formation suppression precedes substantial structural compaction.

2. The dwarf galaxy NGC 185: the flagship of Andromeda's dwarf galaxies for the study of evolved multiple stellar populations

相关度 9

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, galaxies, star formation, metallicity, morphology
作者：Paolo Ventura, Chiara Ventura, Richard D'Souza, Flavia Dell'Agli, Matteo Correnti, Claudio Gavetti, Marco Tailo, Miriam Di Stefano, Eric Bell
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-04

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Reaching galaxies at 10-15 Mpc with HST and JWST requires star formation history (SFH) diagnostics that do not rely on the faint main sequence turn-off. We investigate whether horizontal branch (HB), red clump (RC), and red and asymptotic giant branch (RGB, AGB) stars can fill this role for dwarf elliptical galaxies, using NGC 185 as a test case. We use stellar evolution models of low- and intermediate-mass stars as inputs to population synthesis simulations that reproduce the colour distribution and luminosity function of stars in the (mF606W-mF814W, mF814W) diagram. The study of the stars populating the HB is used to derive the distance modulus and reddening, the average mass loss suffered by the stars during the ascending on the RGB and the SFH occurred during the ancient epochs, before 7 Gyr ago. The luminosity function of the RC gives us constraints on the recent SFH (<8 Gyr). The distribution of HB stars yields a reddening E(B-V)=0.20 mag and a distance modulus DM=24.10 mag for NGC 185. The average mass lost by low-mass stars during the RGB phase is 0.22 +- 0.01 Msun. Star formation was concentrated at early epochs, with half of the stars formed more than 11 Gyr ago. The morphology of the HB indicates essentially no metallicity spread among stars older than 8 Gyr, whereas the slope of the RGB suggests that part of the stars younger than 5-6 Gyr are slightly enriched in metals. Once RGB mass loss is adequately constrained, evolved stars can provide a reliable SFH for dwarf elliptical galaxies well beyond the reach of MSTO-based methods. We discuss the implications for more distant systems and the remaining uncertainties in the underlying stellar models.

3. Understanding galaxies and the origin of the golden mass through the study of scaling relations across cosmic history

相关度 9

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, galaxy, star formation, stellar mass, stellar population, AGN
作者：C. Tortora, A. Gallazzi, A. Gargiulo, F. La Barbera, N. R. Napolitano, R. Ragusa, S. Zibetti
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-04

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The total mass distribution in galaxies strongly influences star formation (SF), both directly via gas availability and feedback, and indirectly via the dark matter (DM) potential well. Star formation efficiency peaks at a characteristic stellar mass of $5 \times 10^{10} M_{\odot}$, often termed the "golden mass", where feedback mechanisms minimally affect SF. This scale also appears in other scaling relations: correlations between stellar population gradients, DM fraction, and total mass density slope versus stellar mass all show pronounced minima at the golden mass. How, why, and when does this golden mass emerge during galaxy evolution? Early cosmic epochs ($z > 1.5$) remain largely unexplored, yet they probe the formative stages of galaxy mass assembly. They provide the full redshift baseline needed to constrain the buildup of stellar mass and the efficiency of gas-to-star conversion. By combining high resolution, multiplexing, and a wide field of view, SHARP on the ELT will address these questions, delivering sharper and deeper data than JWST. In this SHARP White Paper contribution, we present a scientific case exploiting these unique characteristics. SHARP will enable precise measurements of stellar population gradients, DM fractions, and total mass density slopes. Using the mIFU mode, SHARP will obtain spatially-resolved stellar population and dynamical maps for galaxies at $z > 1.5$. Reconstructing these observables for early- and late-type systems will allow us to trace the emergence of the golden mass across cosmic time, and to decode the impact of galaxy processes (mergers, SN, and AGN feedback) by comparing observations with cosmological simulations. 4. A pocket of oxygen in a near-pristine galaxy at z=4.2: chemical inhomogeneity at 10-parsec scales in LAP2

作者：M. Messa, E. Vanzella, S. Rojas-Ruiz, T. Treu, P. Bergamini, G. Di Rosa, M. Lombardi, P. Rosati, A. Zanella, A. Adamo, A. Bolamperti, F. Calura, M. Castellano, R. Gilli, C. Grillo, U. Meštrić
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-03

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We aim to constrain the oxygen abundance, the ionizing properties, and the spatial structure of LAP2, a strongly lensed (with a magnification $μ$~50) oxygen-deficient galaxy at z=4.2 behind the galaxy cluster Abell 2744. We present new JWST/NIRSpec IFU PRISM observations (~13.6 hr) of LAP2, which we combine with archival JWST/NIRCam imaging. LAP2 is intrinsically extremely faint (M_UV=-12.7) and has a low stellar mass (M~$10^5~M_\odot$), comparable to that of massive star clusters. It consists of a compact core ($R_{eff}$~7 pc) embedded in a more diffuse emission (with $R_{eff}$~20 pc). Strong H$α$ emission (EW$_{\rm rest}\simeq880$ Å, $\logξ_{\rm ion}\simeq25.3$), a blue UV slope ($β_{\rm UV}\simeq-2.5$, down to $\simeq-3.1$ in the core), and a Balmer decrement consistent with no dust attenuation indicate a young ($\lesssim10$ Myr), dust-free burst. [OIII]$λ$5007 is not detected in the integrated spectrum, implying [OIII]/H$β\leq0.61$ ($3σ$) and, using an extrapolated strong-line calibration, $12+\log({\rm O/H})\leq6.4$ ($\lesssim0.5\%\,Z_\odot$). This places LAP2 among the most metal-poor galaxies known. Faint [OIII] emission is, however, detected in the core (with $\rm4σ$ significance), where we derive $12+\log({\rm O/H})\simeq6.6$ ($\sim1\%\,Z_\odot$). This difference can be explained either by local self-enrichment, requiring only $\rm \sim0.2\,M_\odot$ of newly synthesized oxygen, or by a higher ionization parameter in the core. The upper limit on HeII$λ$1640 (EW$_{\rm rest}<39$ Å, $3σ$) does not exclude a contribution from metal-free stars. LAP2 extends the census of spectroscopically confirmed EMPGs to star-cluster masses and to $M_{\rm UV}\approx-13$. Its location in a low-density region supports a scenario in which underdense environments preserve nearly pristine gas well after reionization.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The spectral energy distributions of active galactic nuclei (AGN) provide valuable information on the emission mechanisms taking place in their inner regions. Here we use the James Webb Space Telescope to study the near and mid-infrared spectral energy distributions of seven nearby low-luminosity active galaxy nuclei from 1.7 to 24 $μm$. We cleanly separate the AGN spectrum by performing spatial fits of the two-dimensional images across all wavelengths. We then compare these clean unresolved spectra to aperture-based spectra and show that they effectively remove galaxy contamination. Comparing our infrared measurements to X-ray emission, we find that low-luminosity AGN have enhanced near-infrared emission relative to more luminous AGN, a trend that correlates with radio-loudness, while their mid-infrared-to-X-ray ratios are comparable. A majority of our targets show strong compact silicate emission suggesting the presence of warm dust on parsec scales in these systems. We also quantify the near- and mid-infrared power-law slopes and colors of low-luminosity AGN. In the near-infrared we find AGN of all luminosities have similar spectral slopes and colors, however in the mid-infrared, we find low-luminosity AGN typically have steeper slopes and redder colors than more luminous AGN. Both the strong silicate emission and steep mid-infrared slopes disagree with existing models for low-luminosity AGN spectral energy distributions.

6. Spectroscopy-informed Deep Learning Searches for Photometric Little Red Dots in JWST Surveys

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, NIRSpec, galaxies, high redshift
作者：Chenxuan Zhang, Huanian Zhang, Qingwen Wu
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-04

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Little Red Dots (LRDs) are a population of compact, red sources discovered at high redshift with JWST that exhibit distinctive V-shaped spectral energy distributions (SEDs). Existing LRD samples are typically identified using a variety of color-based selection criteria, which can lead to differences in the populations recovered by different studies. We develop a cross-modal machine-learning framework that combines matched NIRCam imaging and NIRSpec/PRISM spectroscopy to transfer spectroscopic information into imaging-based representations, enabling LRD identification from JWST/NIRCam imaging alone. Applied to spectroscopically observed high-redshift galaxies previously classified as non-LRDs, our model identifies 35 new LRD candidates. The majority satisfy at least one independent LRD color selection criterion, and among the 13 candidates with medium- or high-resolution NIRSpec grating spectra, 10 exhibit broad H$α$ emission - a hallmark of LRDs. We further apply the classifier to public NIRCam imaging from the Dawn JWST Archive and two independent JWST surveys, yielding 357 and 23 additional LRD candidates, respectively. The candidates recovered by our method span a broader range of observed SED properties than those in existing samples, suggesting that current color-selected samples may not fully capture the diversity of LRD-like systems present in JWST surveys. Our findings highlight the value of spectroscopy-informed photometric classification as a powerful strategy for systematically mining large-area JWST imaging datasets and provide a practical framework for future studies of this emerging population.

7. ReveaLLAGN 2: Exploring the Nuclear Gas and Stellar Kinematics of Nearby LLAGNs with JWST/NIRSpec IFU

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, galaxies, AGN
作者：Antoine Dumont, Nadine Neumayer, Anil C. Seth, Torsten Böker, Jonelle L. Walsh, Micheal Eracleous, Luis C. Ho, Kameron Goold, David Ohlson, Timothy A. Davis
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-03

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Low-luminosity active galactic nuclei (LLAGN) represent the most common mode of black hole accretion in the local Universe, yet their nuclear structure, stellar and gas kinematics remain poorly constrained at parsec scales. We investigate the nuclear regions of eight nearby representative LLAGN, the ReveaLLAGN sample: M87, M104, M94, M81, NGC1052, NGC4258, NGC5128 and NGC4395. Using JWST/NIRSpec data, we probe the stellar, molecular hydrogen, hydrogen recombination, and high ionisation coronal lines kinematics and their spatial distribution, exploring how detectability and spatial distributions of these lines vary across different Eddington ratios. The stellar LOSV maps reveal a slowly rotating disk in the nuclei of all our targets, characterised by an anti-correlation with the h3 Gauss-Hermite moment. Across the sample, the stellar velocity dispersion rises toward the central black hole. Only in M104 and M94 does the velocity dispersion display a more intricate pattern that departs from a simple disk configuration, indicating the presence of multiple kinematic components in these galaxies. Across the sample, the gas distribution and kinematics exhibit rotation that is frequently decoupled from the stellar rotation, suggesting that the stellar gravitational potential is decoupled from the current accretion state. The gas kinematics are often visually aligned with the radio jet, presenting broader velocity dispersions along the jet direction. Line-ratio maps indicate that the dominant excitation mechanism is photoionisation by the central AGN, with mechanical heating from shocks becoming important only at higher Eddington ratios. Across the sample, we detect coronal lines in all LLAGN when using nuclear-aperture spectra, and we can obtain spatially resolved kinematics only in NGC5128 and NGC4395, the highest Eddington ratio sources.

1. Local Pitch Angles of Galactic Structures in Nearby Galaxies with JWST: II. Dust Lanes and Gas Flows in Bar-Dominated Systems

相关度 9

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, MIRI, galaxies, morphology
作者：Stella Faria-Costa, Irapuan Rodrigues, Ivânio Puerari
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-06

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present a spatially resolved study of local pitch angles in six barred spiral galaxies observed by PHANGS--JWST, focusing on bar-dominated regions where dust lanes are the main structures. We combine a two-dimensional Fourier transform (2D-FT) with a circular-window correlation method to map pitch angles across multiple wavelengths and spatial scales, extending our previous analysis of spiral arms and interarm substructures in disk-dominated galaxies. The dust lanes show relatively large pitch angles, typically $|α|\simeq35^\circ$--$70^\circ$, well above those measured for spiral arms. In several galaxies, the pitch angle changes systematically along the dust lane, with smaller values in the inner regions and larger values farther along the bar, tracing variations in local curvature. The multi-scale analysis shows that larger windows recover the overall geometry of the dust lanes, whereas smaller windows reveal local irregularities and secondary structures. MIRI images generally provide the clearest view of the dust morphology and allow a more precise characterization of local pitch-angle variations, while NIRCam offers a complementary view of the underlying stellar distribution. Unlike spiral-arm pitch angles, which are commonly associated with density-wave patterns, the values measured here characterize the local orientation and curvature of dust lanes associated with bar-driven gas flows. Together with the results of our companion study of disk-dominated galaxies, these findings support a unified, scale-dependent framework in which local pitch angles serve as observational diagnostics of distinct dynamical regimes in nearby galaxies.

2. A Comprehensive Analysis of the H$α$ Luminosity Functions at $z\sim 1.3$ and $z\sim 2.0$ in the CANUCS Lensing Fields with JWST NIRISS

相关度 8

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, star formation, emission line
作者：Qianqiao Zhou, Yuxuan Pang, Xin Wang, Karl Glazebrook, Shengzhe Wang, Hang Zhou, Pengfei Ren, Shamuhawu Hailanhazi, Peter J. Watson, Maruša Bradač, Nicha Leethochawalit, Matthew A. Malkan, Themiya Nanayakkara, Xue-Bing Wu, Hu Zhan
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-09

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present H$α$ luminosity function (LF) measurements at redshifts $z\sim1.3$ and $z\sim2.0$. We adopt JWST grism data in five lensing cluster fields and their parallel fields from the CANUCS program: ABELL-370, MACS-J0416, MACS-J0417, MACS-J1149, MACS-J1423. Based on emission lines spectroscopically identified in the F115W, F150W and F200W filters, our final sample consists of 762 H$α$ emitters, including 433 at $1.1<z<1.5$ and 329 at $1.8<z<2.2$. Through detailed effective volume and completeness analysis for each source, we construct the H$α$ LF in two redshift bins. Thanks to the sensitivity of NIRISS WFSS and gravitational lensing magnification, our sample reaches intrinsic H$α$ luminosities down to $L_{\rm Hα}\sim10^{40.6}~\rm erg~s^{-1}$ at $z\sim1.3$ and $L_{\rm Hα}\sim10^{41}~\rm erg~s^{-1}$ at $z\sim2.0$ with completeness larger than 80\%. The larger survey volume and coverage of multiple independent sightlines help mitigate cosmic variance. After accounting for cosmic variance, we constrain the faint-end slopes to be $α_{\mathrm{H}α}=-1.51^{+0.06}_{-0.04}$ at $z\sim1.3$ and $-1.42^{+0.09}_{-0.06}$ at $z\sim2.0$, indicating a mild evolution between the two redshift bins. We find a negligible contribution from bright active galactic nuclei in our sample. We estimate integrated cosmic star formation rate densities of $0.103^{+0.008}_{-0.007}~M_{\odot}~\rm yr^{-1}~Mpc^{-3}$ at $z\sim1.3$ and $0.149^{+0.016}_{-0.013}~M_{\odot}~\rm yr^{-1}~Mpc^{-3}$ at $z\sim2.0$. The emission-line samples presented here will enable further detailed studies of galaxy properties including metallicities. The methodology presented here can be readily applicable to other JWST slitless spectroscopic datasets and future wide-field slitless surveys, including those from Euclid, Roman, and the Chinese Space Station Telescope.

3. Local Pitch Angles of Galactic Structures in Nearby Galaxies with JWST: I. Spiral Arms and Interarm Substructures in Disk-Dominated Systems

相关度 8

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, MIRI, galaxies, galaxy
作者：Stella Faria-Costa, Irapuan Rodrigues, Ivânio Puerari
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-06

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present a spatially resolved study of spiral arms and feather substructures in seven nearby galaxies observed with JWST. Using eight images per galaxy, from four NIRCam and four MIRI filters, we combine a two-dimensional Fourier transform (2D-FT) with a circular-window correlation method to measure both global and local pitch angles. The 2D-FT identifies the dominant azimuthal mode and the corresponding $p_{\rm max}$, allowing us to reconstruct the spiral pattern and define arm and interarm masks. We then apply cosine filters with different pitch angles and window sizes in the $(\ln (R),θ)$ plane to map the pitch-angle distribution across multiple spatial scales. NIRCam images provide the most robust measurements of the main spiral pattern, whereas MIRI data reveal dusty interarm features, particularly feathers, with greater contrast at the smallest window size ($D_w=0.25$). Across the sample, the main arms typically have $|α|\simeq15^\circ$--$30^\circ$, while feathers show systematically larger values, often above $50^\circ$. These results demonstrate that pitch angle is scale dependent and that different structural components within the same galaxy can occupy distinct angular regimes. NIRCam and MIRI therefore offer complementary views of spiral structure, with the former tracing the large-scale stellar backbone and the latter exposing finer dust substructures. Together with the companion study of barred galaxies, this work provides a common framework for using local pitch angles to investigate distinct dynamical environments in nearby galaxies.

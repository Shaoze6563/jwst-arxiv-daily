1. Interpreting JWST UV luminosity functions and angular correlation functions via a "universal language" for high-redshift galaxies

相关度 13

匹配词：JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies, galaxy, high redshift, star formation, stellar mass
作者：Ivan Nikolić, Andrei Mesinger, Steven G. Murray
分类：astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO
首次提交：2026-11-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) found more bright $z\gtrsim10$ galaxies than expected before its launch. No consensus has emerged on whether difficulties in reproducing them stem from missing physics, simplifying assumptions that break down at the highest redshifts, or model mis-calibration. This is complicated because most galaxy models cannot be compared like-to-like, and models invoking different physical processes can make similar predictions for compressed summaries such as ultraviolet luminosity functions (UV LFs). Here we describe galaxy populations in a universal language: a chain of physically and empirically motivated conditional distributions linking halo mass, stellar mass, star formation rate and UV magnitude, defined consistently across models and data. We build a broad prior from fits to five hydrodynamic simulations, which predict very different high-redshift galaxy populations. We infer the parameters of this flexible galaxy model from $z>6$ JWST observations of UV LFs and angular correlation functions (ACFs). Including the mass-complete ACFs disfavors a large scatter around the stellar-to-halo mass relation (SHMR). With simulation-based priors, the star-forming main sequence (SFMS) is described by a star-formation timescale of about one halo dynamical time, and the scatter around the SFMS increases toward lower stellar masses. The SFMS scatter is the main source of stochasticity in UV magnitude at fixed halo mass, accounting for a median $\sim$61% of the variance at $M_{\rm UV}=-20$ (SHMR scatter: $\sim$36%). Bayesian model selection finds no evidence for redshift evolution of the SHMR or for an SFMS slope different from unity. At $z\sim20$, our fiducial model predicts UV LFs orders of magnitude below estimates from photometric candidates. A consensus on the discrepant UV LF estimates at $z\geq14$ is needed to determine whether model extensions are required.

2. Tracing the Luminosity Evolution of Globular Cluster Systems with JWST: MACS J1149.5+2223 at z=0.544

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, galaxy, stellar population
作者：Jessica M. Berkheimer, Rogier A. Windhorst, William E. Harris, Timothy Carleton, Rafael Ortiz, S. P. Willner
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-16

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present a study of the compact-source population associated with the massive galaxy cluster MACS J1149.5+2223 at z=0.544 using deep JWST/NIRCam imaging in F115W, F150W, and F200W. After photometric and morphological selection, we identify 1,202 unresolved compact-source candidates. The observed luminosity distribution samples only the bright side of the expected globular-cluster luminosity function (GCLF), remaining several magnitudes above the turnover. Based on published GCLF turnovers for Abell 2744 and MACS 0417, we adopt an expected turnover magnitude of m_0=33.66 mag for MACS 1149. A forward-modeled fit then yields sigma=1.71 mag, which characterizes the observed bright-side slope under the adopted turnover rather than the intrinsic width of the full GCLF. Comparison with PARSEC simple stellar population models shows that the elevated luminosities of the compact sources are consistent with the expected fading of younger stellar populations as they evolve toward the present epoch. The brightest sources reach luminosities comparable to those of ultra-compact dwarfs. However, stellar-population degeneracies and possible foreground contamination make it difficult to distinguish. Together with recent JWST studies of globular cluster systems across a range of redshifts, MACS 1149 extends the emerging observational sequence of compact stellar systems and supports stellar population fading as an important contributor to their luminosity evolution across cosmic time.

分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Inspection of deep JWST images centered on the lensing cluster of galaxies MACS J0454.1-0300 reveals a small, well resolved stellar system that is clearly in the foreground. Photometric measurements including a color-magnitude diagram and radial profile show that it is a nucleated dwarf early-type galaxy (dE,N) at a distance of ~60 Mpc and with structurally normal properties. This object (J04:54:07.39-02:59:22.5, referred to as Orion I for shorthand) may be an outlying member of a previously known small galaxy group, or may be simply an isolated field galaxy depending on the accuracy of its distance estimate. The discovery of Orion I suggests that other small stellar systems in the nearby universe might be found through a search of the many deep \jwst imaging fields that are now being accumulated.

4. Unveiling the Multi-Phase Outflow in NGC 4151 with JWST Observations - I. Integrated Properties of the Outflow

分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-11-15

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The biconical outflow of the nearby Seyfert 1.5 AGN NGC 4151 has been studied across the electromagnetic spectrum, yet mapping its multiphase structure with a single facility has remained challenging. Leveraging the broad wavelength coverage of the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST), we present new near- and mid-infrared observations tracing the ionization stratification of these outflows with a unified dataset. We analyze emission lines originating from molecular gas to highly ionized plasma and analyze the ionized gas using a 5-component Cloudy photoionization model covering three primary phases. Applying the footprint line method to characterize the X-ray emitting gas, we find that it holds the largest share of the total mass, suggesting that it is a primary candidate for driving AGN feedback in this system.

## 1. Tracing the Epoch of Reionization through extreme emission line galaxies around Cosmic Noon

- **相关度：** 13
- **匹配词：** JWST, NIRSpec, galaxies, reionization, emission line, AGN
- **作者：** M. Delgado Mancheño, J. Gallego, S. Pascual, M. Tang, R. Pello, N. Laporte, A. Camps-Fariña, M. Chamorro-Cazorla
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-10
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14332) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14332v1)

Star-forming galaxies at z>6 are thought to be the main drivers of cosmic reionization, but their faintness makes detailed study challenging even with JWST. Extreme Emission Line Galaxies (EELGs) are compact, low-mass systems with intense nebular emission and are potential lower-redshift analogues of this population. We aim to assemble and characterize a statistically significant sample of EELGs in the CANDELS/EGS field around cosmic noon, and to assess their validity as analogues of the galaxies that drove reionization. We select EELG candidates from CANDELS photometry with a J-band color-excess criterion that identifies both H$β$+[OIII] and H$α$ emitters. Physical properties are taken from CANDELS data products, and the selection is validated with MMT/MMIRS, Keck/MOSFIRE, HST/WFC3 and JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy, complemented by our own GTC/OSIRIS+ program. We identify 102 H$β$+[OIII] emitters at $z\sim1.7$ and 127 H$α$ emitters at $z\sim1.0$. They are compact ($\sim$1.6 kpc), low-mass ($\log(M_\star/M_\odot)\sim8$) and strongly star-forming (sSFR $\sim10^{-8}$ yr$^{-1}$). Estimated purities of $84.3\pm1.5$\% (H$β$+[OIII]) and $76.8\pm2.0$\% (H$α$) agree with spectroscopic confirmation rates (85\% and 63\%), with no evidence of significant AGN contamination. A joint Bayesian fit, corrected for completeness via a truncated likelihood, yields $\log(M_\star/M_\odot) = (6.71\pm0.16) + (3.31\pm0.46)\log(1+z)$, suggesting an evolution in the characteristic mass of the sample with cosmic time. We spectroscopically confirm a highly pure, AGN-free sample of cosmic-noon EELGs. While their mass scale evolves with redshift, their properties consistently match those of z>5 JWST extreme emitters, supporting them as intermediate-redshift analogues.

## 2. Tracing early galaxies back in time: implications for the dearth of galaxies beyond z~15

- **相关度：** 9
- **匹配词：** James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies, galaxy, high redshift, star formation, stellar mass
- **作者：** L. Ciesla, C. Carvajal-Bohorquez, D. Elbaz, G. Aufort, O. Ilbert, S. Finkelstein, A. M. Koekemoer, P. Oesch, M. Boquien, C. Gruppioni, N. Laporte
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-10
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13835) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13835v1)

Although the James Webb Space Telescope has pushed the observational frontier to $z\sim14$, the onset of star formation in the earliest galaxies remains beyond direct reach, making the star formation histories (SFH) of observed galaxies a key tool for probing earlier epochs. We develop and validate a new SFH model based on an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, capable of reproducing the bursty star formation expected in high-redshift galaxies. Tested on the SPHINX$^{20}$ mock catalogs, it accurately recovers stellar masses and SFRs and reconstructs cumulative SFHs at population level; the formation redshift is well constrained at $z\gtrsim9$ but not at $z\sim8$. Applied to 400 galaxies at $9<z<15$ from the UNICORN/GOODS-South sample, we find that sources observed at $z<10$ typically reached 20% of their stellar mass no earlier than $z\sim12$, disfavoring a clear evolutionary link to spectroscopically confirmed galaxies at $z\gtrsim13$. Among 218 galaxies at $10<z<15$, only 28 had formed 20% of their stellar mass by $z=15$, with a median formation redshift $z_{\rm form,20\%}=16.9^{+1.1}_{-1.1}$, and 24% of our sample had already reached $10^7$M$_{\odot}$ by $z=15$. At $z=17$, only 11 of these 28 galaxies had reached 20% of their stellar mass. The derived survey areas required to detect them are consistent with or slightly more optimistic than previous constraints at the bright end, and larger than some literature estimates at the faint end. At $z=20$, a single galaxy had formed 20% of its stellar mass, yielding a tentative constraint of one object at $M_{\rm UV}=-18.1\pm0.6$ per $\sim200$\,arcmin$^2$. Our results suggest a relatively slow decline in star formation activity between $z\sim15$ and $z\sim17$, followed by a more rapid decline up to $z\sim20$.

## 3. Intermediate-redshift slow rotators as the progenitors of the most massive galaxies at z $\sim$ 0

- **相关度：** 9
- **匹配词：** COSMOS, galaxies, galaxy, star formation, stellar mass, stellar population, quiescent, morphology
- **作者：** C. Muñoz López, Davor Krajnović, B. Epinat, T. Contini, I. Pessa, J. Pharo, A. de Araujo Carvalho, A. R. Chougule
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-09
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13225) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13225v1)

We study the resolved stellar kinematics and star formation histories, and their dependence on the environment, in a sample of 218 intermediate redshift (0.1 < z < 1) galaxies located in the CANDELS and COSMOS fields, observed with VLT/MUSE. The sample covers galaxies with stellar masses ranging M_star ~10^8 - 10^12 M_sun, including both star-forming and quiescent systems. For 113 galaxies, found in dense environments of galaxy groups, we derive the spin parameter ($λ_{R}$), while for the rest of the sample, we use data from previous papers in this series. Given that the surveys used in this work are flux-limited, the $λ_{R}$ distribution of our full sample lacks the bimodality observed in the local Universe, but the subsample of galaxies at z < 0.6 resembles the galaxy population in the nearby Universe, in terms of mass, sizes, and stellar population properties, more than galaxies at z > 0.6. We classified the galaxies into fast and slow rotators, finding that ~8% (17/218) of the galaxies are slow-rotating. The fraction of slow rotators does not change significantly down to z = 0, but we find that in the z ~ 0 sample, the spin parameter is, on average, higher than at higher redshifts. In our subsample at z > 0.6, we observed evidence for the existence of the kinematic morphology-density relation. The slow rotators in our sample mostly assemble their stellar mass in one or two main episodes of formation, and they finished assembling their mass via star formation by redshift of z $\sim$ 1. It is however, not clear if the loss of angular momentum can be associated with the last star formation episode. Based on their location in the mass - size diagram, the intermediate-redshift galaxies are consistent with being the progenitors of the most massive slow rotators observed at z~0, where the subsequent growth is facilitated through gas-poor (dry) mergers.

## 4. Revealing Hidden Excitation in the Core of NGC 4696: New Infrared Diagnostic Diagrams with JWST

- **作者：** Olivia R Pereira, Julie Hlavacek-Larrondo, Michael Reefe, Gary J Ferland, Hyunseop Choi, Mathieu Marquis, Jorge Barrera-Ballesteros, Benjamin Vigneron, Ming Sun, Rebecca E A Canning, Gregory Taylor, Loic Albert, Francesco D&#x27;Eugenio, Megan Donahue, Andrew C Fabian, John S Gallagher, Marie-Lou Gendron-Marsolais, Pierre Guillard, Minghao Guo, Nina Hatch, Ralf Kotulla, Yuan Li, Roberto Maiolino, Allison Man, Michael A McDonald, Brian R McNamara, Valeria Olivares, Marine Prunier, Christopher S Reynolds, Carter Rhea, Annabelle Richard-Laferriere, Helen R Russell, Philippe Salome, Prathamesh Tamhane, Auriane Thilloy, Grant R Tremblay, G Mark Voit, Stephen A Walker
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.HE
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-09
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13116) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13116v1)

The warm molecular and ionized gas in filamentary nebulae of cool-core BCGs provide a sensitive probe of the poorly understood exchange of energy between the ICM, cold molecular gas, and AGN feedback. Optical line ratios commonly yield similar LINER-like classifications across these systems, but do not resolve how the excitation of warm molecular gas varies between compact nuclear structures and surrounding filaments. We present JWST/NIRSpec integral-field spectroscopy of the central $3''\times3''$ ($620\times620~{\rm pc^2}$) of the Centaurus cluster BCG, NGC~4696. The NIR data resolve H$I$ and ro-vibrational H$_2$ emission across compact nuclear apertures, a circumnuclear swirl, and filamentary structures. H$_2$ excitation diagrams yield characteristic low-excitation temperatures of $T_{\rm ex}\simeq1.8-2.2\times10^3$~K, while curvature at higher energies requires hotter or non-thermal components. The H$_2$/Pa$α$ balance varies strongly: filamentary regions have H$_2$ 1-0 S(1)/Pa$α$ ratios 4-8 times higher than the compact nuclear apertures. In contrast, matched-aperture optical diagnostic diagrams show substantially less regional separation, with all regions occupying a low-ionization locus. We thus introduce new spatially resolved NIR diagnostic diagrams that reveal distinct molecular excitation conditions across the nucleus, swirl, and filaments. Ratios are broadly consistent with contributions from AGN-related photoionization, ICM-related cooling radiation, and non-radiative heating, although the present data do not uniquely determine their relative importance. These results demonstrate how spatially resolved NIR spectroscopy reveals excitation variations hidden in conventional optical classifications, providing a new route to constrain the multiphase cooling-feedback cycle in cluster cores.

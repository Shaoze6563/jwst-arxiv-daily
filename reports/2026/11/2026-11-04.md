## 1. Tiny Galaxies, Mighty Nitrogen: Linking Chemical Anomalies to Compact Galaxies at High Redshift using JWST/JADES

- **相关度：** 12
- **匹配词：** JWST, JADES, NIRSpec, galaxies, galaxy, high redshift, morphology
- **作者：** Urvi Thakurdesai, Xihan Ji, Yuki Isobe, Roberto Maiolino, Vasily Belokurov, Courtney Carreira, Brant Robertson, Will McClymont, David Puskas, Andrew Bunker, Stephane Charlot, Mirko Curti, Qiao Duan, Francesco D&#x27;Eugenio, Maria Koller, Jonah Powley, Pierluigi Rinaldi, Jan Scholtz, Charlotte Simmonds, Sandro Tacchella, Hannah Ubler
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03799) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03799v1)

The origin of anomalous chemical enrichment in high-redshift galaxies discovered by JWST remains a key question in galaxy evolution. Enhanced nitrogen abundances have been identified in several galaxies at early cosmic times, but the physical conditions driving this enrichment, and their connection to galaxy morphology, remain unclear. We test whether morphologically compact galaxies at 4 < z < 7 show stronger chemical anomalies than extended systems. Using JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopic data, we construct samples of compact and extended galaxies and analyse their rest-frame spectra. We stack the spectra of each population and measure key UV and optical emission lines. We detect nitrogen emission in the compact galaxy stack, including NIV], and [NII], while also detecting key carbon and oxygen lines. The detection of [OIII]λ4363 enables a direct-Te determination of the oxygen abundance. From these measurements, we infer chemical abundance ratios, including log(N/O) and log(C/O). We find that compact galaxies exhibit elevated log(N/O) (by ~ 0.7 dex) and log(C/O) (by ~ 0.5 dex) abundance ratios relative to extended systems. The abundance patterns of compact galaxy stacks are comparable to those of previously identified individual nitrogen-enhanced galaxies at high redshift and second-generation globular-cluster stars in the Milky Way. These results suggest a possible link between galaxy compactness and chemical enrichment pathways in the early Universe. Deeper JWST NIRSpec IFU observations of compact high-redshift galaxies will be crucial for determining the origin, spatial distribution, and evolutionary significance of these abundance patterns.

## 2. Mass-Metallicity Relation of the Fossil Disk in Six Nearby Galaxies using JWST RGB Stars

- **相关度：** 11
- **匹配词：** JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, NIRCam, galaxies, metallicity
- **作者：** Avinash Ck, Divakara Mayya, Alessandro Bressan, Jairo Andres Alzate Trujillo, Léo Girardi, Shaunak Mishra, Bolivia Cuevas-Otahola
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03961) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03961v1)

We here present the first ever mass-metallicity relation (MZR) of fossil disk populations using the resolved stellar populations in the James Webb Space Telescope Near-Infrared Camera (JWST/NIRCam) images of six nearby galaxies. Both the mass and metallicity were determined using the Red Giant Branch (RGB) stars, employing methods that have been well established to study the halo populations of nearby galaxies using the Hubble Space Telescope images. Specifically, we obtained the mass and metallicity by comparing the color, magnitude and number density near the tip of the RGB (TRGB) stars in the F115W$-$F200W vs F200W color-magnitude diagrams (CMDs) with that of the PARSEC isochrones of 4 and 13~Gyr. The RGB metallicities of the disks of nearby galaxies closely match those found in galaxies at redshifts $z>8$, with the metallicity range following a power-law relation with the fossil disk mass. The power-law index of the fossil disk MZR, $γ\approx0.26$, compares well with the typical value found in MZRs obtained using gas metallicities across redshifts. Through this work, we illustrate the utility of JWST/NIRCam images for obtaining the mass and metallicity of the fossil disks of nearby galaxies at precisions that were not possible earlier, which opens up a new method to carry out the near-field cosmological studies.

## 3. How bursty star formation shapes spectral diversity at high redshifts: breaking degeneracies in early galaxy formation

- **相关度：** 10
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxy, galaxies, star formation, emission line
- **作者：** Viola Gelli, Charlotte A. Mason, Daniel P. Stark, Mengtao Tang, Julian B. Muñoz, Zuyi Chen, Ivan Nikolić
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03790) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03790v1)

JWST has revealed an unexpected abundance of z>10 galaxies and striking spectral diversity. Here, we model bursty star formation histories (SFHs) to explore how they shape this diversity and whether spectroscopy can discriminate between explanations for the UV luminosity function (UVLF) excess. We find strong burstiness ($σ_{UV}(M_h=10^{10}M_\odot)$~0.9 mag), increasing toward low halo masses, is required to reproduce the z~6 UVLF and observed fractions of galaxies in recent star formation upturns and downturns, traced by SFR$_3$/SFR$_{50}$. This burstiness naturally reproduces UVLFs and SFR upturn and downturn fractions over z~6-11. With increasing redshift, we predict galaxies in recent upturns (SFR$_3$/SFR$_{50}>5$) increasingly dominate the UVLF bright end, reflecting rising accretion rates and burstier low-mass halos. This results in higher median emission-line equivalent widths (EWs), bluer UV slopes and weaker Balmer breaks at fixed $M_{UV}$, while distributions of these properties broaden. We find the downturn fraction (SFR$_3$/SFR$_{50}<0.2$) is particularly sensitive to stochasticity: increasing towards faint $M_{UV}$ if burstiness increases in lower mass halos, and evolving with redshift only if stochasticity does. However, the model underpredicts the z~14 UVLF, so we explore two models which match the UVLF: increased burstiness and increased star formation efficiency. From z=10 to 14, at fixed $M_{UV}$, increased burstiness predicts the downturn fraction increases by ~2x and the weak-emitter fraction (H$β$ EW<50Å) by ~1.5x. By contrast, the increased efficiency model predicts a constant downturn fraction and declining weak-emitter fraction. Overall, our results indicate burstiness is important for explaining spectral diversity and evolution at z~6-14, and that spectroscopic samples spanning a broad $M_{UV}$ range can establish its role in the z~14 UVLF excess.

## 4. Probing the Faint Outskirts of Star-Forming Galaxies with 1&lt;z&lt;9 Using Stacked Images

- **相关度：** 9
- **匹配词：** JWST, NIRCam, JADES, CEERS, PRIMER, galaxies, galaxy, stellar mass, stellar population, dust attenuation
- **作者：** Cheng Jia, Enci Wang, Yangyao Chen, Cheqiu Lyu, Hongxin Zhang, Ziwen Zhang, Jinyang Wang, Jie Song, Bojun Tao, Xiaoxuan Chen, Junsheng Li
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-03
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04605) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04605v1)

We present a systematic study of the faint outer light profiles of star-forming galaxies using deep JWST/NIRCam imaging from the CEERS, JADES, and PRIMER surveys. Our sample consists of 14,812 face-on disk galaxies at $1<z<9$ with $\log(M_*/M_{\odot})>8.5$, selected from the DAWN JWST Archive. To characterize the low-surface-brightness outskirts beyond the effective radius ($R_e$), we stack galaxies in bins of stellar mass and redshift, accounting for cosmological surface-brightness dimming, angular-size scaling, and PSF convolution, and estimate residual backgrounds from blank-sky stacks. The color profiles generally become bluer toward larger radii, indicating negative color gradients and supporting an inside-out growth scenario. Galaxies with $\log(M_*/M_{\odot}) < 9.5$ at $z>2.5$ exhibit a weak reversal in their outermost regions, suggesting more complex stellar population gradients at early times. The outer light profiles generally exhibit a good exponential decline with radius, and the outer scale lengths measured beyond $2R_e$ show mass and redshift dependences remarkably similar to those of the conventional mass-size relation. This similarity suggests that the extended outer light is closely linked to the same processes governing the growth of the inner galaxy, rather than providing a direct tracer of ex-situ stellar components in the early universe. We also find little wavelength dependence in the outer scale lengths, indicating that dust attenuation likely has a limited impact at these radii. These results provide new constraints on the buildup of extended stellar distributions during cosmic noon and at earlier epochs.

## 5. Ly$α$ in Compact Broad-Line Emitters, Including Little Red Dots, Is More Closely Linked to the Host Galaxy than to the Broad-Line Nucleus

- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03801) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03801v1)

JWST has found many faint broad-line AGN at $z>4$, but it is unclear whether the Ly$α$ we see from them comes from the accreting black hole or from its host galaxy. The answer matters for how these sources are interpreted and for how many ionizing photons they release. We use deep JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy from the DIVER program to measure Ly$α$ in 12 compact broad-line emitters (CBEs) at $z=4.2$--5.5 in GOODS-N. CBEs include little red dots (LRDs), little blue dots, and other broad-line AGN. Six are detected in Ly$α$. Every source shows a broad-line region, yet Ly$α$ does not follow it: the sources with the strongest H$α$ relative to the UV include weak detections and nondetections, and every detected Ly$α$ line (FWHM $\approx300$--1000~km~s$^{-1}$) is narrower than broad H$α$ (1100--3300~km~s$^{-1}$). Instead, Ly$α$ appears to track the host galaxy. We model the AGN-subtracted host with a dust-attenuated SED fit. Including the upper limits, the observed Ly$α$ follows the Ly$α$ production in this host model more closely than it follows H$α$/UV, in both equivalent width and luminosity. Every detection exceeds its host-model value by a factor of 1.5--18, leaving room for a nuclear contribution. The black hole is visible in these sources, but the Ly$α$ we observe is more closely tied to its host. The median effective Ly$α$ escape fraction of the detections is only 0.09, which suggests that ionizing escape from CBEs is typically modest and varies widely.

## 6. 3D dark matter map of the JWST COSMOS-Web field using diffusion-based generative priors

- **相关度：** 7

- **作者：** Diana Scognamiglio, Brandon Zhao, Katherine L. Bouman, Olivier Doré, Caitlin M. Casey, Maximilien Franco, Ghassem Gozaliasl, David Harvey, Olivier Ilbert, Jeffrey B. Jewell, Jeyhan S. Kartaltepe, Anton M. Koekemoer, Gavin Leroy, Richard Massey, Jason Rhodes, Brant E. Robertson, Marko Shuntov, Sune Toft, Greta Toni, Michael A. Troxel
- **分类：** astro-ph.CO, cs.LG
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03786) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03786v1)

Mapping the three-dimensional distribution of dark matter is key to tracing the growth of cosmic structure. Two-dimensional weak lensing mass maps recover its projected distribution, but they blend structures at different distances along the line of sight. We reconstruct the three-dimensional matter distribution in the 0.54 deg$^2$ COSMOS-Web field using deep James Webb Space Telescope imaging, with an effective source density of 121 galaxies arcmin$^{-2}$ We combine a physical weak lensing forward model with a diffusion-based Bayesian generative prior learned from cosmological simulations, yielding a posterior distribution of the matter field across 20 lens planes to $z \simeq 2$, doubling the redshift reach of previous Hubble Space Telescope-based 3D reconstructions and approaching cosmic noon, an epoch of rapid galaxy growth. The reconstruction separates structures that overlap in projection and recovers non-Gaussian matter structure across a range of spatial scales, including over-densities associated with independently identified galaxy clusters that were not detected in earlier weak lensing maps. Posterior sampling quantifies the range of three-dimensional matter distributions allowed by the data, enabling a probabilistic visualization of the cosmic web while propagating uncertainties in its inferred structure. Null and simulation-based tests show that the recovered structures are supported by the lensing signal and are consistent with expectations from the fiducial $Λ$CDM cosmology. These results extend three-dimensional weak lensing mapping into the high-redshift, non-Gaussian regime and establish a route towards probabilistic mapping of the evolving cosmic web with current wide-field lensing surveys.

## 7. Surface Nitrogen enrichment from the LMC to the Early Universe: the roles of rotation, overshooting and mass loss

- **相关度：** 6
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxies, high redshift, metallicity
- **作者：** Gautham N. Sabhahit, Jorick. S Vink
- **分类：** astro-ph.SR, astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.HE
- **首次提交：** 2026-11-02
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03798) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03798v1)

The strong nitrogen (N) features observed in high-redshift galaxies, such as GN-z11, with JWST point to an efficient source of N production in the early Universe. Very massive stars (VMSs) are promising candidates owing to their strong winds and large convective cores of CNO-processed material. Whether massive-star surfaces in general become N-enriched depends on the interplay of convective-boundary mixing (overshooting), rotation, and mass loss, which remains poorly constrained. Using a grid of MESA evolutionary models, we isolate the overshooting and rotation channels at $40$, $100$, and $200\,M_\odot$ and investigate how these channels bring N-rich material to the surface of massive and very massive stars as a function of initial mass and metallicity. We also construct a synthetic Large Magellanic Cloud (LMC) population based on a larger initial mass grid ranging from $20-300\,M_\odot$ for comparison with the observed N-enriched fraction. VMS surface enrichment is remarkably robust across cosmic metallicity. At lower metallicities, wind stripping is weaker and the overshooting channel shuts off entirely, yet a modest amount of rotation of $\sim90\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ alone still enriches the surface. At higher metallicity, stronger winds dominate and uncover the core almost independently of the adopted mixing. The two channels operate through physically distinct mechanisms: overshooting enriches the surface only once winds strip the envelope down into the N-rich layers left by the receding core, giving an abrupt jump. Rotation, by contrast, mixes N outward continuously through the layers above the core, producing a gradual rise. Applied to the LMC, a population with modest rotation drawn from the observed velocity distribution and modest overshooting reproduces the observed N-enriched fraction without large core boundary mixing efficiencies otherwise required by non-rotating models.

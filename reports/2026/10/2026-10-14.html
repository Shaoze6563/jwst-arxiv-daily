1. Mergers may not be the main channel for galaxy stellar mass growth at redshift $z\gtrsim5$: Predictions from cosmological hierarchical models and simulations

相关度 11

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, galaxies, stellar mass, high redshift, cosmic dawn, star formation
作者：Hao Fu, Mohammadreza Ayromlou, Francesco Shankar, Lars Hernquist, Andrea Lapi, Daniel Roberts, Feng Yuan, Maulik Bhatt, Evgenii Chaikin, Simona Gallerani, Luca Graziani, Antonio La Marca, George Mountrichas, Yingjie Peng, Haocheng Wang, Kai Wang
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Mergers are a crucial channel of galaxy growth, but their role relative to star formation remains uncertain at high redshift. Here we investigate merger rates and merger-driven stellar mass assembly for galaxies with stellar mass $M_\star \gtrsim 10^8 \, {\rm M_{\odot}}$ at $z\simeq 5-11$, by combining complementary theoretical approaches within $Λ$CDM. These include the DECODE semi-empirical model, the TNG100 and EAGLE hydrodynamical simulations, and the \textsc{L-Galaxies} semi-analytical model, together with the Millennium and TNG100-Dark simulations used to examine dark matter subhalo statistics and merger timescales. This compares models with different treatments of baryonic physics and galaxy-halo connections. We find that dark matter-only simulations predict merger timescales $\sim 30\%$ longer than those inferred from cosmological simulations including baryons. Galaxy major merger ($M_{\rm \star,sat} / M_{\rm \star,cen} > 0.25$) rates are high at cosmic dawn and increase towards earlier epochs, reaching $\sim 1/{\rm Gyr}$ at $z\sim 5$ and peaking at $\sim 3/{\rm Gyr}$ at $z\sim 8$. Despite these high rates, mergers contribute only $\lesssim 10\%$ of the total cosmic stellar mass density evolution. The ex-situ stellar mass fraction ranges from a few percent to $\sim 20\%$ for all galaxies at $z\gtrsim 5$, across different models and simulations. Their broad agreement implies that stellar mass assembly at cosmic dawn is dominated by in-situ star formation rather than mergers. These findings suggest that the high pair fractions identified by JWST may not directly trace efficient ex-situ stellar mass growth, highlighting the need for physically motivated merger timescales to interpret galaxy assembly in the early Universe.

2. The DAWN JWST Archive: A spectroscopically-informed population study of the physical properties of galaxies at z>2

分类：astro-ph.GA

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Charting the evolution of star-formation, stellar mass and dust build-up over cosmic time relies on the accurate characterisation of the stellar population properties of high redshift galaxies. We present a catalogue of spectroscopically derived physical properties for ~12,000 galaxies at z>2, with archival JWST/NIRSpec PRISM spectroscopy and JWST/NIRCam imaging from the DAWN JWST Archive. We present a custom version of the SED fitting code BAGPIPES, used to perform joint spectrophotometric modelling of PRISM spectra and broadband NIRCam photometry. In doing so, we publicly release a science-ready catalogue of the physical properties of galaxies beyond cosmic noon. We report key physical properties, such as stellar masses, star-formation rates (SFR) and timescales, and V-band dust attenuation (Av). We further probe the recent star-formation histories (SFH) of galaxies via their SFRs on 3 Myr, 10 Myr, and 100 Myr timescales, capturing both rising and falling recent SFHs. Through dust-corrected ugi colour-colour diagrams of massive galaxies, we show how quiescent and dusty galaxies populate this colour space at z>2, with galaxies experiencing a recent downturn in star-formation occupying distinct parameter space. We also study the dustiest galaxies in our sample, showing that they are preferentially more massive and strongly star-forming relative to the parent sample, residing on the high-mass end of the star-forming main-sequence. The slope of the attenuation curve has a strong dependence on Av, varying from steep to shallow as the dust attenuation increases and the specific star-formation rate decreases. Our findings show that the unparalleled number statistics of this catalogue of spectroscopically derived stellar population properties uniquely enables the study star-formation, quenching and dust properties of galaxies from cosmic noon to cosmic dawn.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present the first deep, targeted LOFAR 144 MHz source catalogue of the COSMOS field, together with the first 144 MHz source counts in this field. The data comprise 48 h of observations. Over the 2.6 sq. deg. region adopted for the catalogue analysis, the final Dutch-array image reaches a median rms sensitivity of ~100 uJy/beam at an angular resolution of 15" x 6". The resulting catalogue contains 1984 radio sources; a peak S/N >= 5 selection is adopted for the resolved/unresolved classification and source-count analysis. We characterised the catalogue using Monte Carlo source-injection and recovery simulations, including dedicated simulations of extended sources, to quantify completeness, flux-density redistribution, and morphology-dependent source-recovery effects. The catalogue reaches 90% completeness at 1.48 mJy. The resulting Euclidean-normalised differential source counts are measured down to ~0.4 mJy and show good agreement with LoTSS DR3 144 MHz source counts and with the VLA-COSMOS 3 GHz counts scaled to 144 MHz, with ~5% of catalogue sources having multiple 3 GHz counterparts within a beam-matched search region. Comparison with the VLA-COSMOS 3 GHz catalogue shows good astrometric agreement, with median offsets of Delta_RA = 0.35" (+0.03"/-0.05") and Delta_Dec = -0.83" (+0.02"/-0.03"), approximately 6% of the corresponding synthesised-beam dimensions, and no significant radial dependence across the field. The resulting catalogue provides a low-frequency view of COSMOS and a reference dataset for future deeper LOFAR observations and multiwavelength studies of this extensively characterised extragalactic field.

We present resolved optical integral-field spectroscopy of the 32 luminous and ultraluminous infrared galaxy systems of the Multiphase Analysis of (U)LIRG Nuclear Activity (MAUNA) sample, drawn from the GOALS survey and selected to have JWST/MIRI medium-resolution spectrometer (MRS) observations. The data set combines new Keck/KCWI observations of 10 systems with archival VLT/MUSE cubes for 22 systems, covering H$β$ to [S II] at ~1 arcsecond spatial resolution (~0.9 kpc at the sample median distance). Using the optical [N II]/H$α$ BPT diagram, we find that composite and LINER-like excitation together account for most of the classified area (median fractions 0.44 and 0.26), while Seyfert-like gas covers a median fraction of 0.02 but carries a far larger proportion of the line luminosity where it is present. Both the projected size of the Seyfert-classified region, $R_{\rm Sy}=0.08$-$7.5$ kpc, and its [O III] luminosity increase with the bolometric AGN luminosity, with $R_{\rm Sy}\propto L_{\rm AGN,bol}^{0.54\pm0.18}$, as expected for gas photoionized at a constant ionization parameter. The scatter about the luminosity relation depends on dust opacity: the [O III] luminosity per unit AGN luminosity falls by $1.09\pm0.25$ dex per unit depth of the 9.7 $μ$m silicate absorption feature (Spearman $ρ=-0.60$, $p=0.001$). Optical excitation diagnostics therefore locate AGN-excited gas reliably but systematically underestimate the luminosity the AGN supplies to the host in the most obscured (U)LIRGs.

5. Massive black holes in the first billion years with JWST

相关度 6

匹配词：JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies
作者：Hannah Übler
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The James Webb Space Telescope has unlocked uncharted territory for the study of early black holes, enabling the detection of fainter and smaller black holes at larger cosmological distances than previously possible. Compared to nearby massive black holes, they are characterised by several unexpected attributes, such as their multi-wavelength properties or their relation to their host galaxies. These observational findings challenge our understanding of the past evolution of present-day supermassive black holes, and provide novel anchor points to constrain theoretical models of black hole formation and growth. This review summarises our current understanding of the observed properties of newly discovered, active black holes in the early Universe, discusses the status of mass measurements and the relation to their hosts, as well as open questions and future prospects.

6. JWST Imaging of Edge-on Protoplanetary Disks. V. Flux Ratio Reversal and Disk-edge Structure in the Flying Saucer

作者：Jie Ma, Karl R. Stapelfeldt, Marion Villenave, Gaspard Duchêne, François Ménard, Ryo Tazaki, Youcef I. Bouarour, Greta Guidi, Laurine Martinien, Maxime Roumesy, Christophe Pinte
分类：astro-ph.EP, astro-ph.SR
首次提交：2026-10-12

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present near- and mid-infrared broadband imaging observations of the edge-on protoplanetary disk 2MASS J16281370-2431391, a.k.a. the 'Flying Saucer', obtained with NIRCam and MIRI on board JWST. These data are combined with archival optical and near-IR broadband imaging from HST, millimeter dust continuum emission from ALMA, and optical lightcurves from the K2 and TESS for a comprehensive multi-wavelength study. The JWST observations reveal a reversal of the flux ratio between the two reflection nebulae at near-infrared wavelengths (2-3 $μm$), which can be explained by a misaligned inner disk. For the first time, JWST reveals the bright lining along the disk edge at 2 and 3 $μm$, arising from forward scattering of background photons by dust grains near the $τ\approx 1$ layer. By combining the dust lane thickness measured with HST and JWST, we find the dust grains up to at least $\sim$3$μ$m are vertically mixed throughout the disk and have undergone significant growth. In addition, comparison of earlier HST/NICMOS J and K band images with those from JWST/NIRCam reveals variability in the scattered-light morphology, as well as measurable proper motion of the system with respect to the background stars, confirming that the Flying Saucer is co-moving with the young star population of $ρ$ Ophiuchi.

1. A Comprehensive JWST Slitless Spectroscopic Census of $\sim$12,000 Emission-Line Galaxies at Redshift $0\sim9$ in COSMOS

相关度 13

匹配词：JWST, COSMOS, NIRCam, COSMOS-3D, galaxies, galaxy, emission line, star formation, AGN
作者：Shuqi Fu, Zijian Zhang, Danyang Jiang, Jie Chen, Linhua Jiang, Chenxi Bao, Yihe Wang, Ronaldo Laishram
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-09

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Emission-line galaxies provide key diagnostics of star formation, chemical enrichment, and AGN activity across cosmic time. We present a systematic census of emission-line sources using JWST/NIRCam wide-field slitless spectroscopy (WFSS) from the COSMOS-3D program, which covers a large contiguous area of 0.33 deg$^2$ and provides F444W grism spectroscopy over $3.9$--$5.1\ μ$m. By combining slitless spectroscopy with deep multi-band imaging data, we construct a homogeneous spectroscopic sample of 12,275 objects at redshift 0--9. The typical $3σ$ line-flux limit reaches $3.2\times10^{-18}\ {\rm erg\ s^{-1}\ cm^{-2}}$. To address source confusion inherent to slitless spectroscopy, we develop a multi-step identification framework that combines information from spectra, images, spectral energy distributions, and spatial distributions. We introduce convolutional neural network and large language model to emulate the human identification process and improve efficiency. The resulting catalog provides spectroscopic redshifts, emission-line measurements, and derived physical properties for all sources. The F444W WFSS enables the detection of multiple emission lines, including primarily [O III], H$α$, He I $\lambda10830$, [S III] $λ\lambda9531,9069$, Pa$β$, Pa$α$, Br$β$, PAH, Br$α$, allowing a continuous census of galaxies across cosmic time. We construct composite spectra in several redshift bins and detect a variety of faint spectral features that are difficult to identify in individual spectra, including higher-order hydrogen recombination lines, CO absorption bands, and [Fe II] lines. This catalog provides a key reference dataset for studies of galaxy evolution and AGN activity, and demonstrates the power of WFSS for constructing large, unbiased spectroscopic samples over wide areas. The full catalog will be publicly available online upon publication.

2. When dust growth takes over: the origin of dust in post-reionization galaxies

相关度 11

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, galaxy, reionization, star formation, stellar mass, metallicity
作者：Omima Osman, Gabriella De Lucia, Andreas. L. Faisst, Lizhi Xie, Fabio Fontanot, Michaela Hirschmann
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-08

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The post-reionization epoch ($z\sim4$-6) marks a critical phase in galaxy evolution, during which galaxies rapidly assemble their stellar mass, enrich their interstellar medium, and build up substantial dust reservoirs. The physical processes responsible for such rapid dust enrichment remain uncertain. We investigate the dust enrichment history of the ALPINE-CRISTAL-JWST (ACJ) sample using the semi-analytic galaxy formation model GAlaxy Evolution and Assembly (GAEA), which follows galaxy assembly, chemical enrichment, and dust evolution. We select model galaxies matching the observed stellar masses, star formation rates, and metallicities of the ACJ sample, compare their dust properties with observed scaling relations, and trace their enrichment histories and environments. The model reproduces the observed dust mass-stellar mass relation and the dust-to-stellar mass, dust-to-gas, and dust-to-metal ratios as functions of stellar mass and metallicity. Reproducing these constraints requires efficient dust growth in the interstellar medium; stellar dust production alone cannot account for the observed dust reservoirs, even under the most optimistic assumptions. The combination of stellar mass, star formation rate, and metallicity provides a good predictor of galaxy dust content. Our model suggests rapid dust build-up consistent with the previously noted rapid metal enrichment, with evidence for a transition around $z\sim8$-9 below which dust growth becomes the dominant dust formation channel. Finally, ACJ-like galaxies preferentially reside in locally overdense environments, suggesting that environment may have contributed to their accelerated evolution. Taken together, these results support rapid and coupled stellar, metal, and dust build-up in ACJ galaxies during the post-reionization era.

3. An empirical calibration of the spectral resolution of JWST NIRSpec PRISM

相关度 8

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies, emission line, morphology
作者：Hang Zhou, Xin Wang, Yuxuan Pang, Shengzhe Wang, Karl Glazebrook, Themiya Nanayakkara, Hu Zhan
分类：astro-ph.IM
首次提交：2026-10-09

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present an empirical calibration of the effective spectral resolution of the JWST NIRSpec PRISM disperser based on a large sample of galaxies drawn from archival observations. Our sample spans a wide redshift range, $0.5 \lesssim z \lesssim 10$, enabling a wavelength-dependent characterization of the PRISM resolving power across the full bandpass using the [O III]$λ\lambda4959,5007$ doublets. To ensure robust measurements, we impose strict signal-to-noise requirements on both continuum and emission-line detections. Emission-line profiles are fitted with multi-Gaussian models, and the full width at half maximum (FWHM) is measured for each source. The derived empirical resolution curve shows a systematic offset from the nominal pre-launch estimate, lying between the tabulated instrumental resolution and the point-source shutter limit. We also discuss how source size affects the final spectral resolution, and provide an analytical formula that combines wavelength and morphology dependence in a root-sum-square framework. This work delivers a population-averaged, statistically robust calibration of the effective PRISM spectral resolution for typical galaxy observations. The resulting calibration is directly applicable to statistical analyses of galaxy spectra obtained with NIRSpec PRISM. Our empirical calibration exceeds the pre-launch JDox values by a factor of 1.03 to 1.63, depending on source morphology, and provides the first wavelength and morphology dependent calibration of the NIRSpec PRISM spectral resolution.

4. A dust-reddened quasar in a [C II]-luminous host at z=6.55

Gloudemans, Francesco Guarneri, Bin Jia, Elia Pizzati, Huub Röttgering, Jan-Torge Schindler, Feige Wang, Jinyi Yang
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-09

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Most known quasars at $z>6$ were selected through their rest-frame UV emission. Only a small number of reddened quasars have been identified at these redshifts, leaving their nuclear and host-galaxy properties poorly constrained. We present JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy and ALMA band 6 observations of the quasar J1440+0019 at $z=6.5488$, revealing its dust-reddened nature and jointly characterizing its nucleus and host galaxy. The continuum is best described by an SMC-like extinction law with $E(B-V)=0.20\pm0.03$. Three absorption systems detected in N V, Si IV, and C IV are blueshifted by approximately $900$--$3600$ km s$^{-1}$ and likely trace an ionized nuclear outflow. The broad Mg II line and extinction-corrected continuum yield a black-hole mass of $\log(M_{\rm BH}/M_\odot)=9.04\pm0.42$, a bolometric luminosity of $L_{\rm bol}\simeq1.1\times10^{47}$ erg s$^{-1}$, and an Eddington ratio of $λ_{\rm Edd}\simeq0.8$. ALMA observations further reveal its host galaxy to be among the most [C II]-luminous known at $z>6$, with $L_{\mathrm{[C II]}}=(7.21\pm0.25)\times10^9 L_\odot$. For a fiducial dust temperature of 47 K and emissivity index $β=1.6$, the dust continuum implies $L_{\rm TIR}=8.9\times10^{12} L_\odot$ and $M_{\rm dust}=4.0\times10^8 M_\odot$. The inferred dynamical mass places J1440+0019 in the region of the $M_{\rm BH}$--$M_{\rm dyn}$ plane occupied by high-redshift blue quasars. These observations reveal a moderately reddened, rapidly accreting quasar in a luminous, dust-rich host during reionization. Its measured nuclear and host-galaxy properties provide an observational reference for identifying and characterizing similar systems in future systematic searches with wide-area infrared surveys.

5. A Single Line-Emitter Identified in an Unbiased Search with JWST/NIRSpec: Multi-Object Coronagraphy for Slitless Spectroscopy

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, AGN, emission line
作者：Mason S. Huberty, Claudia Scarlata, Matthew J. Hayes, Alaina Henry, Danielle A. Berg, Vieri Cammelli, Richard Ellis, Anne E. Jaskot, Yu-Heng Lin, Vihang Mehta, Matilde Mingozzi, Pierluigi Monaco, Alberto Saldana-Lopez, Aayush Saxena, Jonathan C. Tan, Alice Young
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-08

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We report the detection of a previously unknown object with a single emission line within the Hubble Ultra Deep Field, using a new JWST NIRSpec technique called Multi-Object Coronagraphy for Slitless Spectroscopy (MOCSS) with the G140M/F100LP observing mode. The MOCSS technique involves opening almost all the shutters on the NIRSpec microshutter array, while simultaneously closing the shutters that lie on top of known low redshift objects, facilitating a wide field slitless spectroscopic search without contamination from bright objects. This observational technique has enabled the detection of a strong emission line in four different dither positions and in two position angles, and thus cannot be artificial. This line is detected at 1.60 $\rm μm$ with a line flux of $[2.5\pm 0.3]\times10^{-18} \rm erg\ s^{-1} cm^{-2}$ and a FWHM of $13.2\pm2.0$Å, and does not correspond to any known source with some of the deepest HST and JWST imaging in existence. Based upon the absence of imaging detection, we place a lower limit on the observed frame equivalent width (EW) of 7620Å on this object, which strongly limits the number of possible lines that this line can be. Given the wavelength, an EW limit that is near the ceiling of expectation, and the lack of other lines in the bandpass, this line cannot be easily explained by [OII] at $z=3.29$, [OIII] at $z=2.20$, H$α$ at $z=1.44$, and Ly$α$ at $z=12.16$. We discuss possible AGN and transient phenomena that could explain the finding.

6. The road to $H_0$, $Ω_m$, and $w$ with a global neutrino-telescope network

相关度 5

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, high redshift
作者：Pavel Zhelnin, Gonzalo Herrera, Nicholas Kamp, Carlos A. Argüelles
分类：astro-ph.CO, hep-ph
首次提交：2026-10-08

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The newly measured correlation between the neutrino and X-ray luminosities of Seyfert galaxies has yielded the first neutrino-based measurement of the Hubble constant. Building on this result, we forecast the reach of a global neutrino-telescope network by 2030, utilizing full-sky IceCube exposures combined with KM3NeT-ARCA and Baikal-GVD. We find that this 2030 configuration sharpens the neutrino-measured Hubble constant from today's $H_0 = 66^{+44}_{-37}$ to $H_0 =66^{+15}_{-13}$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$. Additionally, we project that the disk-corona slope of the neutrino X-ray luminosity relation ($L_ν\propto L_X^β$) will reach $β=0.72\pm0.04$, enabling a $\geq \!5σ$ exclusion of the calorimetric value $β=1$. Finally, we show that, if a high-redshift population of neutrino sources, such as JWST's "little red dots," is identified and obeys the same luminosity relation, we can obtain a new independent measurement of the matter density $Ω_m$ and the dark energy equation-of-state parameter $w$.

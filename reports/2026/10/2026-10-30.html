1. Simulation-Informed Dust Masses in Typical $6<z<14$ JWST Galaxies

相关度 9

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, high redshift
作者：Jed McKinney, Dhruv T. Zimmerman, Desika Narayanan, Caitlin M. Casey, Julian B. Munoz, Paul Torrey, Hui Li, Federico Marinacci
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-27

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Direct detections of dust in typical $z>8$ galaxies remain untenable, despite the presence of dust suggested by rest-frame UV/optical spectro-photometry. Informed by cosmological zoom-in simulations where dust particles evolve on-the-fly, we present a novel method for inferring dust masses in high-redshift galaxies using stellar half-light radii and UV/optical attenuation. Our methods reproduce observed trends at lower redshift, ultimately diverging from Milky Way-based calibration factors by up to $\sim1.5$ dex. We apply this method to observed JWST galaxies from $6<z<14$, and find dust masses in the range $M_{\rm dust}\sim10^3-10^5\,M_\odot$ that are fully consistent with upper limits from ALMA, and suggest an early dust enrichment period by supernovae followed by efficient grain destruction in the hot gas. Future observations may use our tabulated calibration to infer dust masses over a wide range of stellar masses and redshifts.

2. PRISMS GHZ8: Evidence for a Young Burst and Potential Merger Activity at z=10.23

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, MIRI, galaxy, galaxies, reionization, star formation, stellar mass, stellar population, SED fitting
作者：Ruqiu Lin, Jorge Zavala, Javier Álvarez-Márquez, Luis Colina, Marco Castellano, Adriano Fontana, Paola Santini, Lorenzo Napolitano, Sarah Kendrew, Alejandro Crespo Gómez, Victoria Salazar, Abdurro'uf, Stefano Carniani, Tiger Hsiao, Yuichi Harikane, Mahmoud Hamed, Takuya Hashimoto, Akio Inoue, Danial Langeroodi, Rui Marques-Chaves, Ikki Mitsuhashi, Yurina Nakazato, Borja Pérez-Díaz, Pierluigi Rinaldi, Carlota Prieto-Jiménez, Ana Varo-O'Ferrall, Fang-Ting Yuan, Shuairu Zhu, Zhen-Ya Zheng
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-29

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

UV-bright galaxies in the early Universe provide important constraints on the rapid build-up of stellar mass and the sources responsible for cosmic reionization. However, the physical processes that drive their high UV luminosities remain unclear. Here we report JWST/MIRI LRS spectroscopy of GHZ8, a UV-bright galaxy at z=10.23 with a magnification $μ=1.23$. The spectrum shows a $\sim 5σ$ detection for the $\rm Hβ$-[OIII] complex and $\sim 4σ$ for $\rm Hα$ line, with fluxes of $3.2\pm0.9\times10^{-18}$ and $\rm 1.7\pm0.4\times10^{-18}\,erg\,s^{-1}\,cm^{-2}$, respectively. Based on JWST/NIRCam imaging, we find that GHZ8 consists of two components separated only by 0.16" ($\approx600$ pc): a bright, extended main component and a faint, compact southeastern clump, hereafter referred to as GHZ8-main and GHZ8-SE. Through morphological decomposition, we find demagnified $M_{UV}=-19.91\pm0.10$ and $-18.03\pm0.15$ mag for GHZ8-main and GHZ8-SE, with blue UV slopes of $β_{UV}=-2.53\pm0.22$ and $-3.45\pm0.49$, respectively, indicating that both components host young stellar populations. In particular, the extremely blue UV continuum of GHZ8-SE requires an extremely young, ISM-poor stellar population with a high ionizing photon escape fraction, $f_{esc}= 0.6-1.0$. Combining the UV and $\rm Hα$ luminosities with the results from the spectro-photometric SED fitting, we constrain the star-formation activity of GHZ8-main, and find either a recent, short-lived burst of star formation that is now in its final phases, or continuous star formation over a relatively short period ($<50\,{\rm Myr}$). GHZ8 therefore provides observational evidence for bursty star formation in a UV-bright galaxy. Globally, GHZ8 may be undergoing a merger-induced starburst, although deeper imaging or spatially resolved kinematic information is required for a more definitive interpretation.

3. A GLIMPSE of Dark Matter: JWST Constraints on Early Structure Formation

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies, reionization
作者：Julian B. Muñoz, John Chisholm, Seiji Fujimoto, Tiger Yu-Yang Hsiao, Kristen B. W. McQuinn, Priyamvada Natarajan, A. Saldana-Lopez, Hakim Atek, Arghyadeep Basu, Danielle Berg, Ryan Endsley, Lukas J. Furtak, Damien Korber, Rohan P. Naidu, Richard Pan, Daniel Schaerer
分类：astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.GA, hep-ph
首次提交：2026-10-27

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

While we have a solid understanding of how dark matter (DM) behaves during recombination ($z\sim 10^3$) and in the late universe ($z\lesssim$ a few), much less is known about intermediate times, when the first galaxies formed. These galaxies provide a powerful handle on DM through structure formation, as halos have to exist to host luminous sources. Here we use recent determinations of the UV luminosity function from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) GLIMPSE lensing program, along with legacy Hubble and Subaru data, to test the small-scale behavior of DM during the epoch of reionization ($z\sim 7$). Marginalizing over astrophysical uncertainties, we find the matter power spectrum to be consistent with the cold-DM prediction down to sub-Mpc scales, with 1$σ$ uncertainties of 0.2 dex at $k=3-9 \, \rm Mpc^{-1}$ and 0.5 dex at $k=9-27 \, \rm Mpc^{-1}$, the smallest scales probed at these early times. For thermal-relic warm DM we constrain $m_{\rm WDM} > 3.2\, \rm keV$ (95% CL), independent of lower-$z$ probes. Our results test small-scale structure formation in a new redshift window and demonstrate the promise of JWST to constrain the nature of DM.

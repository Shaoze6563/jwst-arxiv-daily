1. Galaxy Luminosity Functions at z~7-10 from Four Years of JWST Observations

相关度 8

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies
作者：Bangzheng Sun, Haojing Yan
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-23

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present galaxy luminosity functions at $z\approx 7.4$ and 10 based on a comprehensive search for candidate galaxies at $6.4\lesssim z\lesssim11.3$ in JWST's public NIRCam data four years after its operation. Our work utilises nine fields covering $\sim$1,045~arcmin$^2$ in total, including seven blank fields and two galaxy cluster fields. Using the dropout technique, we selected 3,733 dropouts from the F090W passband and 2,396 dropouts from the F115W passband, which constitute the largest samples of candidate galaxies at $z\approx 7.4$ and $z\approx 10$ to date. Among these objects, 338 F090W-dropouts and 99 F115W-dropouts already have secure redshifts from JWST NIRSpec spectroscopy over four years, which we use to form two large spectroscopic subsamples to assess the magnitude-dependent contamination rates of their parent dropout samples. Our luminosity functions are based on the results after statistically correcting for both incompleteness and contamination. We find that the luminosity function at $z\approx 7.4$ can be well described by a conventional Schechter function but with a faint-end slope of $α=-1.70\pm0.16$ that is significantly shallower than most of the previous studies at $z\approx 7$--8. To verify this result, more imaging and spectroscopy at $\sim$30~mag will be needed. The luminosity function at $z\approx 10$, on the other hand, can hardly be fitted by a Schechter function but is more consistent with a power law in the form of $Φ(M)\propto 10^{-0.4(β+1)M}$, with the best-fit slope of $β=-2.36\pm0.16$. While such a behavior is largely driven by the high number densities at $M_{\rm UV}\lesssim -21.8$~mag, it is supported by the currently existing spectroscopic confirmations. Further spectroscopy at the bright end($\lesssim 26$~mag) will be needed to strengthen this result.

2. Star clusters at cosmic dawn I: the emergence of the initial cluster mass function

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, galaxies, cosmic dawn, star formation
作者：Fred S. Thompson, Julien Devriendt, Adrianne Slyz, Taysun Kimm, Cheonsu Kang
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-23

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

JWST observations of lensed galaxies reveal unusually bright star-cluster candidates within the first billion years after the Big Bang. However, the physical processes shaping the initial cluster mass function (ICMF) remain uncertain. We study the formation of a galaxy embedded in a dark matter halo whose virial mass reaches $10^{10} \mathrm{M}_\odot$ at $z=6$, using cosmological radiation-hydrodynamics zoom-in simulations. Its star clusters, represented by sink particles, accrete gas and form stellar populations that supply radiation and supernova feedback, thereby regulating their own growth. As we do not prescribe an efficiency per free-fall time or a fixed star-cluster formation duration, the ICMF emerges self-consistently. We assess the effects of numerical resolution and stellar radiation. Schechter fits to the central-galaxy ICMF give truncation masses near $6 \times 10^6 \mathrm{M}_\odot$, with substantial uncertainty. At fixed resolution, including stellar radiation makes the low-mass slope of the ICMF shallower, from $-1.60$ to $-1.34$, while doubling the spatial resolution steepens it back to $-1.54$. Stellar radiation also reduces the abundance of low-mass clouds, particularly those with lower gas densities, and shortens the formation time of star clusters to about a Myr, i.e. well before their first supernovae go off. On average, more rapidly fed star clusters accrete for longer, making their final masses rise faster than linearly with the initial accretion rate and contributing to a flatter ICMF. At fixed host-galaxy star formation rate, our brightest simulated clusters reach UV luminosities comparable to those of observed lensed star-cluster candidates.

3. Local Analogs of Little Red Dots: Clues to the X-ray Weakness

相关度 6

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, high redshift, AGN
作者：Shi-Jiang Chen, Jun-Xian Wang, Zhen-Ya Zheng, Ruqiu Lin, Luis C. Ho, Zhen-Yi Cai, Chang-Hao Chen, Zsofi Igo, Jia-Lai Kang, Ruancun Li, Teng Liu, Wenke Ren, Zhen-Bo Su
分类：astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.HE
首次提交：2026-10-23

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

A major question about JWST's little red dots (LRDs) at $z\sim4-8$ is the origin of their apparent X-ray weakness. Even deep Chandra stacks yield only tentative detections or broad-band limits and cannot distinguish among super-Eddington accretion, intrinsic X-ray weakness, and Compton-thick (CT) obscuration. Local analogs offer a powerful complementary route. At $z\sim0.3$, similarly luminous sources require $\sim10^{3-4}$ less exposure for comparable signal-to-noise, suffer less source confusion, and directly probe the diagnostic rest-frame soft X-rays. Here we present a pilot Einstein Probe/FXT study of six "local little red dots" (LLRDs), V-shaped broad-line green pea galaxies selected as low-redshift counterparts of LRDs. These sources show X-ray diversity: "X-normal" LLRDs resemble type 1 AGNs in X-ray luminosity and optical variability, whereas the "X-weak" stack lies $\sim0.5-0.7$ dex below standard unobscured-quasar scaling relations while remaining compatible with the representative LRD stack and 95% of published individual limits. The X-weak LLRDs also lack detected optical variability and have redder optical continua than X-normal LLRDs, making their V-shaped SEDs closer to those of high-redshift LRDs. The detection of the X-weak stack at $\sim3.3σ$ with the soft-X-ray-sensitive EP-FXT provides rest-frame $\sim2-3$ keV constraints difficult to reconcile with a typical unobscured super-Eddington model or a local-AGN-like CT geometry examined here, although modified variants remain possible. The simplest alternative is a mildly X-ray-weak corona ($Δα_{\mathrm{ox}}\sim-0.1$ to $-0.2$) with Compton-thin absorption ($N_{\mathrm{H}}\sim10^{22-23}$ cm$^{-2}$), consistent with the independent Balmer decrement/break diagnostics. Together with growing samples of local LRD analogs, our results offer a practical path for testing the physical nature of LRD X-ray weakness.

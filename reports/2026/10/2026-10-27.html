1. Recently launched AGN-driven outflow in a lensed quiescent galaxy at Cosmic Noon

相关度 13

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies, AGN, quiescent, star formation
作者：Letizia Bugiani, Andrew B. Newman, Sirio Belli, Cosimo Marconcini, Pietro Bergamini, Sai Gangula, Claudio Grillo, Andrea Bolamperti, Katherine E. Whitaker, Aliza Beverage, Richard S. Ellis, Amir H. Khoram, Allison Matthews, Marco Lombardi
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-23

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We analyze JWST/NIRSpec IFU spectroscopy of MRG-M0150, a gravitationally lensed, massive quiescent galaxy at $z=2.6$ from the INQUEST-JWST survey. Thanks to the combination of lensing magnification with JWST's angular resolution, we are able to build spatially resolved maps of the ionized gas spatial distribution, kinematics, and ionization properties in this system. We detect ionized gas emission throughout the galaxy: the lack of residual star formation inferred from line-ratio diagnostic diagrams, together with the decoupling between the gas and stellar kinematics, suggest that the ionized gas is in a wind. We use the 3D gas kinematics modeling code \texttt{MOKA3D} to constrain the geometry and physical properties of the ionized gas wind. We find that the observations are consistent with a biconical wind driven by the low-luminosity AGN at the center of the galaxy, which is detected in X-rays. The wind reaches high velocities of $1000-1400$ km/s, implying that part of the gas may escape the halo. The ionized gas mass outflow rate, $(0.6 \pm 0.2)$~${\rm M}_{\odot}$~yr${}^{-1}$, is modest but exceeds the upper limit on the residual star formation rate, and it is likely that the wind is multiphase and carries more mass. We show that typical unresolved observations would not detect the wind, suggesting that the incidence of such outflows may be higher than previously recognized. Simple geometric arguments show that the observed outflow must have been launched $\approx 0.45$~Myr ago from the time of observation. This evidence suggests that low-level AGN activity is an important mechanism for maintaining quiescence in massive galaxies at cosmic noon, potentially over cosmological timescales.

2. How Quickly Did the Giants Form? Constraining the Formation Timescales of Massive Quiescent Galaxies at Cosmic Noon Using Chemical Evolution Modelling

相关度 11

匹配词：NIRCam, galaxies, quiescent, high redshift, star formation, stellar mass, spectral energy distribution, SED fitting, DSFGs, DSFG, Dusty star-forming galaxies
作者：Patrick N. O'Connor, J. Trevor Mendel, Sven Buder, Melissa K. Ness, Claudia del P. Lagos, Arjen van der Wel
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-26

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Massive quiescent galaxies (MQGs; $M_\star \sim 10^{11} \, \mathrm{M_\odot}$) at cosmic noon ($z \sim 2$) host $α$-enhanced stellar populations ($\mathrm{[Mg/Fe]} \sim 0.4 - 0.5$) at near-solar [Fe/H], implying rapid formation histories that spectral energy distribution (SED) fitting alone constrains poorly. To determine the physical conditions and formation timescales that generate these abundances, we employ a one-zone chemical evolution model with nucleosynthetic yields calibrated against resolved stars in the Sculptor dwarf spheroidal. Comparing our model to a compilation of observed abundances, we find MQGs are consistent with rapid formation timescales of $Δt_\mathrm{SF} \sim 0.1 - 0.4 \, \mathrm{Gyr}$. The progenitors must sustain high star-formation efficiencies (approximately the molecular gas depletion time; $τ_\star \sim 0.05 \, \mathrm{Gyr}$) and retain most of their metal-enriched ejecta ($η\sim 0$), consistent with high-redshift dusty star-forming galaxies (DSFGs). However, our model favours moderately active progenitors with typical star-formation rates (SFRs) of $200-400 \, \mathrm{M_\odot \, yr^{-1}}$, matching recently identified `NIRCam-dark' galaxies more closely than the broader DSFG population. Sustained starbursts ($\mathrm{SFR} \gtrsim 1000 \, \mathrm{M_\odot \, yr}^{-1}$) are disfavoured, since they would form most stellar mass before significant SN Ia enrichment and underproduce the observed [Fe/H]. The same framework also reproduces the central abundances of local early-type galaxies over longer timescales ($Δt_\mathrm{SF} \sim 0.6 - 1 \, \mathrm{Gyr}$). Chemical abundances can therefore provide powerful constraints on the formation histories of MQGs, and incorporating them into SED-fitting routines will ensure inferred histories remain consistent with the observed chemistry.

Heavily dust-obscured active galactic nuclei (AGN) are crucial for understanding supermassive black hole growth. Using multi-band MIRI photometry from the MINERVA survey, we report the discovery in the UDS field of an extremely mid-infrared bright Compton-thick AGN (CT AGN) at $z \sim 3.8$, \textit{el Ascua} (the Ember). The AGN and/or its host galaxy is detected at 870\,$μ$m with ALMA and at 1.4\,GHz with the VLA. With UV-to-radio spectral energy distribution modeling, we estimate that the dusty host galaxy has a stellar mass of $\log\left( M_\star / M_\odot \right)=10.82 \pm 0.57$ and a star formation rate of ${\rm{SFR}} =157 \pm 8 \,M_\odot\,{\rm yr^{-1}}$, and we confirm the presence of a powerful dusty AGN with an intrinsic accretion luminosity of $\log\left( L_{\rm{acc}} /{\rm erg\,s^{-1}} \right)=47.12 \pm 0.58$. CT obscuration is revealed by comparing the upper limit on the X-ray luminosity over $2-10 \,{\rm{keV}}$ with the $6 \,{\rmμ{m}}$ mid-IR AGN luminosity. We additionally find evidence for a radio excess by comparing its total IR-to-radio ratio to those of typical star-forming galaxies and radio-quiet AGN, implying that \textit{el Ascua} could be a rare radio-loud CT AGN. With an extremely high ratio between black hole accretion rate and SFR, the AGN is likely nearing the peak of a deeply obscured phase of black hole growth before AGN feedback blows away the obscuring material. Our work demonstrates the power of MIRI to reveal heavily obscured AGN missing from our supermassive black hole census.

4. How Steep is your Dust? Direct Measurements of Nebular Attenuation Curves at Cosmic Noon with JWST

作者：Gabriel Maheson, Sirio Belli, Sandro Tacchella, Letizia Bugiani, Joel Leja, Rebecca Davies, Minjung Park, Amir H. Khoram, Matteo Sapori, Caterina Liboni, Benjamin P. Collins
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-23

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Tracing the shape of the nebular attenuation curve, which is crucial for accurately measuring intrinsic emission-line fluxes, has proven challenging both in the local universe and at high redshift. Here we present nebular dust attenuation curves measured directly for 24 star-forming galaxies ($9.3<\log(\rm M{\star}/M_\odot)<11.1$) at cosmic noon ($1.8<z<3.3$) from the Blue Jay survey. Using deep JWST/NIRSpec spectra, we measure up to eight hydrogen recombination lines from H$δ$ to Pa$β$ per galaxy and find little diversity in the curve shape, with most galaxies agreeing with the \citet{calzetti_dust_2000} attenuation curve. For over $90\%$ of the sample, the nebular reddening from the Balmer lines is consistent with that from the Paschen lines. Using \texttt{Prospector} spectral energy distribution (SED) fits, we obtain the stellar reddening and find that the difference between stellar and nebular reddening increases with stellar mass, and that each reddening measurement is independent of inclination, which disagrees with the two-component dust model. We then measure the impact of using the derived nebular attenuation curve or a literature curve when determining metallicity using the strong-line method, and find that for some diagnostics (e.g., S32, S3, S23, and S3O3), the difference can be as large as $5σ$. Caution is needed when using these diagnostics if the nebular attenuation curve cannot be directly measured, since the resulting uncertainty can exceed the measurement uncertainty on the emission-line fluxes even with high signal-to-noise Balmer decrement measurements. These results show that the shape of the nebular attenuation curve is poorly understood and that constraining it is important for reducing systematic uncertainty in galaxy measurements.

We present a stacked JWST/NIRSpec spectrum of 104 galaxies at $λ_{\rm rest}=1200\unicode{x2013}2000\;\mathrm{\mathring{A}}$ to study nebular emission and absorption from the interstellar medium (ISM) and stellar winds. The sample is drawn from known [O III] emitters with mean redshift $z=6.50$, absolute UV magnitude $M_\mathrm{UV}=-20.7$, and stellar mass $M_{\star}=10^{8.93}\;\mathrm{M_\odot}$. The stacked continuum closely resembles lower-redshift Lyman Break Galaxy composites, with a few notable differences. Ly$\;α$ has comparable strength and is redshifted relative to the [O III] systemic frame, consistent with production in gaseous outflows. High-ionization emission lines including O III], C III], and He II are slightly stronger than in lower-redshift stacks but exhibit narrow widths, disfavoring ionization from a broad-line AGN. Emission-line ratios slightly favor ionization by a hard stellar continuum, though a contribution from a narrow-line nuclear source cannot be strictly excluded. Numerous strong absorption lines at both high and low ionization are blueshifted by $\sim200\;\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, again indicating outflows driven by stellar winds or entrained metal-enriched ISM. A simple model suggests that at $z\sim6.5$ most star-forming galaxies are too young for winds at these velocities to propagate beyond $\sim3\unicode{x2013}9$ proper kpc, implying that the observed [O III] emitter population alone cannot explain the frequency of heavy-element absorbers seen in quasar spectra. In this picture, early enrichment of the intergalactic medium likely arises from undetected low-mass galaxies ($M_\star\sim10^5\unicode{x2013}10^6\;\mathrm{M_\odot}$) clustered around the more massive systems detected in JWST/NIRCam WFSS surveys.

We present the Star-formation from Photometry through the Addition of Medium-bands (SPAM) survey, a 62.9 hr JWST/NIRCam imaging program to add ten new filters, nine medium-band filters, and one broad-band filter to the CEERS legacy dataset. 7. Multiphase Gas Structure in JWST Little Red Dots

相关度 7

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, high redshift
作者：Seok-Jun Chang, Max Gronke, Jorryt Matthee, Charlotte Mason
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-25

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

JWST has uncovered a population of compact high-redshift galaxies, 'little red dots' (LRDs), exhibiting broad Balmer emission lines that often show narrow absorption features and strong Balmer breaks - spectral signatures attributed to dense gas surrounding rapidly growing black holes, but whose small-scale structure remains unknown. A striking feature of LRD spectra is that the H$β$ absorption is systematically deeper than H$α$, yet in any homogeneous gas this ordering is physically impossible. Here we model the H$α$ and H$β$ line profiles using radiative transfer simulations for 11 LRDs with JWST sensitive high resolution spectroscopy, and independently constrain the $n=2$ hydrogen column density, $N_{\rm HI,\mathit{n}=2}$, from the Balmer-break continuum. We find that the $N_{\rm HI,\mathit{n}=2}$ from H$α$ in a homogeneous medium cannot reproduce the observed H$β$ absorption in several objects, and that this $N_{\rm HI,\mathit{n}=2}$ typically falls two orders of magnitude below the lower bound set by the Balmer break. Both tensions are simultaneously resolved by a multiphase medium of dense clumps within diffuse hot ionized gas. This multiphase gas structure is a necessary ingredient for any viable LRD model, regardless of the nature of the central engine.

RR Lyrae (RRL) stars and Type II Cepheids (T2Cs) are key standard candles and tracers of old stellar populations. The deep, wide-field capabilities of facilities such as the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) and the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope will significantly expand their utility as a Population II alternative to Classical Cepheids for distance scale calibrations. We present theoretical pulsation properties-including light-curve morphologies, amplitudes, and multi-filter relations for RRLs (Z = 0.0001 - 0.02, M = 0.51 - 0.80, Msun) and T2Cs (Z = 0.0001 - 0.01, M = 0.495 - 0.65, Msun) to enable detailed studies of Galactic and extragalactic populations. Using alreday available non-linear convective pulsation models, we converted their predicted bolometric light curves across various chemical compositions and evolutionary stages into the JWST and Roman photometric systems. From these, we derived multi-filter Period-Amplitude and Color-Color diagrams, alongside Period-Luminosity and Period-Wesenheit relations with explicit metallicity terms. RRL relations display a significantly stronger zero-point metallicity dependence than those of T2Cs, despite sharing similar slopes. Consequently, T2Cs offer a distance scale indicator that is remarkably robust against metallicity uncertainties. As a preliminary test, we applied our metal-dependent Period-Wesenheit relations to a subset of JWST-observed RRLs in the WLM galaxy, deriving a distance modulus of mu_0 = 24.85 +/- 0.03 mag (assuming the literature average metallicity). The derived distance to WLM is consistent with most literature values, though slightly shorter than determinations based on Classical Cepheids and the Tip of the Red Giant Branch. These theoretical relations pave the way for future high-precision distance determinations and asteroseismic diagnostics with JWST and Roman.

分类：astro-ph.GA

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Lyman Alpha Emitting galaxies (LAEs) are young, actively star-forming galaxies identified by their strong Lyman-alpha emission at a rest-frame wavelength of 121.6 nm, making them valuable tracers of galaxy evolution and the large-scale structure of the Universe. The One-hundred-deg^2 DECam Imaging in narrow-bands (ODIN) survey collects LAE samples during Cosmic Noon. Because LAE selection is based on photometric measurements derived from source-detection algorithms, the choice of detection method can directly affect the resulting LAE samples. We therefore compare two prominent source detection methods, Source Extractor (SE) and Tractor (Tr), to determine which is better suited for LAE selection in ODIN. We investigate differences between SE and Tr in the ODIN-COSMOS field, focusing on the z=3.1 LAE sample. We find that a significant fraction of Tr LAEs have low continuum flux measurements, increasing the inferred narrow-band excess and resulting in an LAE sample nearly twice as large as SE. However, LAEs detected by Tr but not SE exhibit equivalent width estimates indicative of potential contamination. We introduce an additional LAE selection criterion based on the expected Lyman-break behavior of LAEs at z~3 to reduce low-redshift contamination without requiring a significant detection of the break itself. Our results demonstrate that differences in source detection can significantly affect narrow-band LAE selection and motivate the use of SE photometry for ODIN LAE catalogs.

We present the discovery of supernova (SN) Ares, a strongly lensed supernova at $z_{\rm spec}=1.276\pm0.002$ in a massive, triply imaged host galaxy behind the MACS J0308.9+2645 galaxy cluster. JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy spanning the rest-frame optical to NIR of Ares reveals helium features at $5876\,\mathring{\mathrm{A}}$, $7065\,\mathring{\mathrm{A}}$, and $10830\,\mathring{\mathrm{A}}$, as well as H$α$ and Pa$β$. A comparison to local core-collapse SN spectra and light curves leads to a classification of SN Ares as a Type IIb supernova, the highest-redshift member of its class spectroscopically identified. A comparison to local SN IIb light curves provides a phase estimate for the spectrum of $34_{-9}^{+10}$ rest-frame days post-radioactive peak, providing a unique view into a high-redshift stripped-envelope supernova in its late-photospheric evolution. A lens model of the cluster constructed using recent JWST data indicates that SN Ares is the first image to arrive and will reappear in two future images near the center of the cluster plane in $\gtrsim 70$ years, with predicted magnifications of $\sim5\times$ and $\sim1.5\times$. Planned JWST/NIRSpec Multi-Object Spectroscopy of the cluster will allow for more detailed modeling of the lensed system for tighter constraints on the time delay and magnifications, as well as a template spectrum at the location of SN Ares.

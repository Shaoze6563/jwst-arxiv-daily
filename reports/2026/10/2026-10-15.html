1. IRAC-Excess Galaxies in the NIRSpec Wide Survey: physical properties of bright EELGs at $z\sim7$ and implications for Reionization

相关度 13

匹配词：NIRSpec, JWST, galaxies, reionization, star formation, AGN, emission line
作者：Katherine Ormerod, Renske Smit, Joris Witstok, Anna de Graaff, Michael V. Maseda, Andrew J. Bunker, Chris Willott
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present an analysis of 23 Spitzer/IRAC excess galaxies at $5.66 < z < 7.64$, observed as part of the NIRSpec Wide GTO survey. With NIRSpec PRISM spectroscopy, we measure a median rest-frame [OIII]$+\rm{H}β$ EW $=1583$~Å, consistent with predictions from the pre-JWST selection and we identify $17/23$ $(\sim74\%)$ of our sample as extreme emission line galaxies (EELGs), based on a selection of [OIII]$+\rm{H}β$ EW $\geq1000$~Å. The typical EELG is powered by star-formation rather than active galactic nuclei (AGN) based on their line widths, line ratios and absence of high-ionization lines, though we cannot exclude an AGN contribution in a few individual sources. We find that our luminous ($\langle M_\mathrm{UV}\rangle=-20.7$) EELG sample is characterised by sub-solar metallicities $(7.38 < \rm 12 + \log(O/H) < 8.12)$, young stellar populations ($t_\star \sim 20$ Myr), and enhanced ionizing photon production efficiencies, with median $\log_{10}(ξ_\mathrm{ion}/\mathrm{erg}^{-1}~\mathrm{Hz}) = 25.62$. We find that more UV luminous ($\sim L_\mathrm{UV, z=7}^\ast$) EELGs exhibit systematically lower O32 ratios than fainter ($\sim 0.1\times L_\mathrm{UV}^\ast$) EELGs at fixed [OIII]$+\rm{H}β$ EW, indicating less extreme radiation fields and/or lower gas densities than their fainter counterparts. Furthermore, bright EELGs exhibit low neutral hydrogen column densities, consistent with a scenario in which they have largely ionized their circumgalactic medium (CGM). Despite this, we find very low Lyman-$α$ escape fractions ($<2$\%), suggesting these systems reside in relatively small ($\lesssim 0.25$~pMpc) ionized regions of the intergalactic medium (IGM), possibly suggesting that bright galaxies have a minor contribution to reionization.

2. Galactic Outflows and Star-Formation Quenching: A JWST Perspective

相关度 11

匹配词：JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, galaxies, star formation, AGN, quiescent
作者：Rebecca L. Davies, Sirio Belli
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Outflows driven by active galactic nuclei (AGN) are widely considered a key mechanism for regulating and quenching star formation in massive galaxies. However, direct observational evidence linking outflows to quenching has remained elusive for decades. This article reviews recent findings for massive galaxies at cosmic noon ($z\sim 2$), based on sensitive James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) observations tracing both star formation histories (through Balmer absorption lines) and neutral outflows (through blueshifted NaID absorption). Neutral outflows are found in $\sim$ 25 - 50% of massive ($\log(M_*/M_\odot)>$ 10) galaxies, independent of star formation rate. Outflows from star-forming galaxies are consistent with being powered by star-formation and/or AGN activity, whereas outflows from quiescent systems are generally attributed to current or previous AGN activity. There is growing evidence that recent rapid quenching of star-formation is associated with powerful AGN-driven outflows with mass loading factors $η\gtrsim$ 1 - 10. The neutral gas dominates the outflow mass budget but in most cases its velocity is below the halo escape velocity, indicating that this gas may form part of a galactic fountain. Detections of neutral gas inflows and residual cold gas reservoirs in quiescent galaxies suggest that other feedback mechanisms such as AGN heating are required to keep galaxies quenched over long timescales. We highlight the potential of multiphase outflow measurements to constrain feedback models, describe the main systematic uncertainties in mass outflow rate measurements, and discuss how the $z\sim 2$ results inform our understanding of the formation of the earliest massive quiescent galaxies.

3. How JWST is Revolutionizing Our View of Early Galaxy Formation

相关度 11

匹配词：JWST, James Webb Space Telescope, galaxy, galaxies, reionization, cosmic dawn, star formation, stellar mass
作者：Rychard Bouwens
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has been revolutionizing our understanding of early galaxy formation over its first four years of science operations. With un- precedented sensitivity, spatial resolution, and wavelength coverage from the near- to mid-infrared, JWST has enabled astronomers to identify hundreds of star-forming galaxies at redshifts z>~8 (within ~650 million years of the Big Bang) through strong spectral breaks and characterize these galaxies through sensitive spectroscopy. These capabilities have provided new insights into the stellar populations, neutral gas, nebular continuum emission, chemical enrichment, dust content, and ionization conditions of galaxies during cosmic dawn, revealing more numerous and brighter systems than anticipated from pre-JWST models. We discuss the implications of these discoveries for reionization, the buildup of stellar mass and metals, and the evolution of galaxy populations and luminosity functions. JWST is also revealing a new physical picture of early galaxies, characterized by compact, clumpy star formation, high gas fractions, and intense radiation fields. Despite much progress, many open questions remain, including the efficiency, intermittency, and stellar initial mass function (IMF) of early star formation, conditions in the interstellar medium (ISM), masses, and structure of the earliest galaxies, and the topology of reionization. Resolving these open questions will require lensing magnification, sensitive spectroscopy to > 5 micron, including with the Atacama Large Millimeter Array (ALMA), exploration to higher redshifts, and larger, more complete samples, helping to clarify how the first galaxies formed and evolved.

分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

JWST observations of massive quiescent galaxies (MQGs) at $z\sim2$ have revealed diverse star formation histories (SFHs). In this letter, we investigate whether these variations are linked to the environment in which MQGs reside, as quantified by the host dark matter halo mass. We identify 12 MQGs from a representative sample of galaxies at $1.6<z<3.5$ obtained by the Blue Jay survey, a JWST Cycle 1 program, and infer their SFHs via modelling of the deep NIRSpec medium-resolution spectra along with multi-band photometry. By correlating the formation and quenching epochs of individual MQGs with their respective host halo masses from the 3D-HST survey, we find that after accounting for the redshift range of the sample ($1.8<z<2.5$), MQGs in more massive haloes formed at significantly earlier cosmic epochs. Meanwhile, although these galaxies also quenched earlier overall, the scatter in quenching epochs across similar halo masses indicates that the quenching process likely involves other contributing factors and is not solely determined by halo mass.

5. Sleeping or Dead? Probing the Quenching Mechanism of JWST Dormant Galaxies at z > 3 with ALMA

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

JWST has revealed a population of "dormant'' galaxies at z>3 with strong Balmer breaks and weak emission lines, whose star formation appears to have declined only recently. Whether these galaxies are undergoing a lasting transition toward quiescence or merely experiencing a temporary suppression of star formation remains unclear. We investigate their molecular gas content using archival ALMA observations of [CII]158um and CO rotational transitions. Starting from all publicly available JWST/NIRSpec prism observations and using joint spectrophotometric fitting, we construct a parent sample at z>3 and log(Mstar/Msun)>9.5 in the ALMA-accessible COSMOS, UDS, and GOODS-South fields. Our final sample comprises 7 dormant galaxies and 15 quiescent galaxies with ALMA constraints on their molecular gas content (mostly 3 sigma upper limits). We find that dormant galaxies exhibit systematically lower star formation rates averaged over the past 10Myr (SFR10Myr) than over the past 100Myr (SFR100Myr), supporting a recent rapid decline in their star formation. For the ~70% of the sample with sufficiently constraining ALMA observations, their molecular gas fractions lie at least ~0.3-2dex below those expected for main-sequence galaxies. Moreover, the molecular gas fractions of dormant galaxies show no systematic difference from those of quiescent galaxies in our sample. This indicates that dormant galaxies have already reached a gas-poor state shortly after quenching and favors a scenario in which the rapid shutdown of star formation is associated with depletion or removal of the molecular gas reservoir, rather than primarily with suppressed star formation efficiency while retaining a substantial molecular gas reservoir. Our results suggest that, if no substantial gas replenishment occurs in the future, dormant galaxies are more consistent with becoming "dead'' than with being temporarily "sleeping''.

6. Large-scale Environmental Dependence of AGN Activity Revealed by JWST Pa$α$ Emitters

相关度 10

匹配词：JWST, galaxy, galaxies, AGN, star formation
作者：Danyang Jiang, Linhua Jiang, Shuqi Fu, Zijian Zhang, Jie Chen, Chenxi Bao, Yihe Wang, Junya Arita, Darshan Kakkad, Jan-Torge Schindler
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The role of the large-scale environment in regulating AGN activity remains an open question. We investigate the Mpc-scale environments of 70 AGNs at $z=1.1$-$1.5$ using a highly complete sample of 3,419 Pa$α$ emitters with accurate spectroscopic redshifts from JWST slitless spectroscopic survey. This dataset allows us to robustly trace the environments and quantify the environmental dependence of AGN properties. We measure the eighth-nearest-neighbor surface density of Pa$α$ emitters around each AGN, probing $\sim$1.5 pMpc scale environments. We find that this environmental density exhibits a weak positive correlation with radio luminosity. Radio-bright AGNs tend to reside in statistically denser environments than their radio-faint counterparts. The environmental density also has a positive correlation with the Eddington ratio and a weak negative correlation with the central black hole mass, implying more efficient black hole accretion in dense environments. We construct Pa$α$ luminosity functions (LFs) in AGN environments and find that they are systematically elevated relative to the field Pa$α$ LF. In addition, their faint-end slopes are shallower, suggesting accelerated galaxy growth and enhanced quenching of star formation. As Pa$α$ emitters trace gas-rich, star-forming galaxies, our results support a scenario in which the large-scale cold gas promotes AGN activity at cosmic noon.

7. Hot Molecular Hydrogen in Galaxies at Redshift ~ 0.2-1.5 Revealed by JWST

相关度 10

匹配词：JWST, galaxies, galaxy, star formation, AGN
作者：Jie Chen, Linhua Jiang, Shuqi Fu, Zijian Zhang, Danyang Jiang, Jing Wang, Ran Wang, Darshan Kakkad
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Molecular hydrogen (H$_2$) is the fundamental component of the molecular gas reservoir that fuels star formation and black hole growth. While prominent hot H$_2$ emission ($\gtrsim 1000$ K) has been explored in nearby galaxies, its excitation mechanisms at higher redshifts remain poorly constrained. We present a systematic study of hot molecular gas traced via ro-vibrational H$_2$ emitters at z~0.2-1.5 using JWST slitless spectroscopy. Exploiting JWST's great sensitivity to near-infrared H$_2$ ro-vibrational transitions such as H$_2$ 1-0 S(1), we build a sample of 144 H$_2$-luminous galaxies and characterize their multi-wavelength properties. About 96.5% and 80% of these galaxies are detected in the radio and far-infrared (FIR) bands, respectively, and their hot H$_2$ emission strongly correlates with the radio and FIR radiation. Based on radio excess, X-ray emission, and optical diagnostics, 62 sources (43%) exhibit significant AGN activity. The H$_2$ line luminosities of the sample show a sublinear dependence on radio luminosity, while the relation between the H$_2$ luminosities and total infrared luminosities is approximately linear after excluding the radio-excess sources. These scaling relations suggest that star formation provides a baseline contribution to hot H$_2$ excitation, while radio-excess sources require an additional contribution from AGN-related processes such as jet- or wind-driven shocks. These results reveal a multi-channel excitation scenario and shed new light on the interplay between star formation, AGN activity, and the interstellar medium during galaxy evolution.

We combine wide-area COSMOS-3D observations with deeper GOODS-N data from JWST/NIRCam F444W wide-field slitless spectroscopy to trace cosmic star formation over $z\sim0$--9. The observations cover approximately $0.33~\mathrm{deg}^2$ in COSMOS and $60.9~\mathrm{arcmin}^2$ in GOODS-N, with typical $3σ$ integrated line-flux limits of $3.2\times10^{-18}$ and $1.04\times10^{-18}~\mathrm{erg\,s^{-1}\,cm^{-2}}$, respectively. The common $3.9$--$5.0\,μ$m wavelength window probes Br$α$, Br$β$, Pa$α$, Pa$β$, H$α$, and H$β$ across the local Universe, cosmic noon, and reionization. Our final sample contains 9146 galaxies, including 7883 in COSMOS and 1263 in GOODS-N. COSMOS constrains luminous emitters, while GOODS-N extends the luminosity functions toward fainter sources. We measure dust-corrected line luminosities, account for line-detection completeness, and convert the six lines to equivalent H$α$ luminosities assuming Case B recombination. Schechter fits reveal an increase in the characteristic H$α$-equivalent luminosity toward cosmic noon. Integrating the resulting star formation rate functions above $0.24\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}$, we trace the cosmic star formation rate density from the local Universe to reionization. The combined Brackett, Paschen, and Balmer measurements are broadly consistent with the canonical UV- and IR-based cosmic star formation history at $z\lesssim6$. Fixed-slope tests show that the H$β$ estimate at $z\sim8$ remains sensitive to the external photometric faint-end constraint and extrapolation below our spectroscopic limit. These measurements provide complementary constraints on the distribution and integrated rate of star formation across cosmic time.

作者：Dhruv T. Zimmerman, Jed McKinney, Desika Narayanan, Caitlin M. Casey, John Chisholm, Alex M. Garcia, Michelle C. Jecmen, Jessica Kelley-Derzon, Federico Marinacci, Julian B. Muñoz, Laura V. Sales, Ethan Savitch, Paul Torrey
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-14

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Correctly modeling the dust attenuation law in galaxies is essential to properly inferring their growth and physical properties. With the rapid discovery of $z>6$ galaxies by JWST and lack of constraints on the properties of dust at these redshifts, we must turn to theoretical models to predict the properties of dust at these redshifts. In this paper, we develop a theoretical foundation for the nature of high-redshift attenuation laws. We analyze $107$ cosmological zoom-in simulations of galaxies run with the SMUGGLE galaxy formation model. SMUGGLE includes an explicitly modeled multiphase interstellar medium and a state-of-the-art dust model with a self-consistently evolving dust grain size distribution. We forward model these simulations with the radiative transfer package POWDERDAY to produce synthetic spectra and attenuation curves for a set of $z=5-15$, $M_*\sim10^{6.5}-10^{10}\; \mathrm{M}_{\odot}$ galaxies. We find evidence for flatter attenuation curves than the commonly used locally-calibrated literature attenuation laws assumed when modeling high-redshift galaxies. The vast majority of our attenuation curves also lack the 2175 Angstrom absorption feature. Our attenuation laws do not evolve strongly over $z=15-5$, which we attribute to relatively constant fractions of small grains up to $z=4$ in our simulations. We present a set of simulation-informed priors on dust attenuation law parameterization that could be implemented in SED fitting codes. There is an anti-correlation between galaxy mass and attenuation curve slope and positive correlation between galaxy mass and $A_V$, which we attribute to dust growth becoming efficient in our massive galaxies with $\log{M_*/\mathrm{M}_{\odot}}\gtrsim9$ and with ISM conditions above a critical metallicity threshold.

10. The connection between massive galaxies and their dark matter haloes from $z=0.25$ to $z \sim 4$ from the combined COSMOS, XMM-LSS and ECDF-S fields

匹配词：COSMOS, galaxies, galaxy, stellar mass
作者：Natalia Stylianou, Matt J. Jarvis, Rohan G. Varadaraj, Peter W. Hatfield, Joel Hamlett, Catherine L. Hale, Tariq Yasin
分类：astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Understanding how galaxies populate and grow within dark matter haloes is essential for linking the observed evolution of galaxies to the hierarchical growth of structure. We present a galaxy clustering analysis using optical and near-infrared data from the UltraVISTA and VIDEO surveys covering $9.3$ deg$^2$ across the COSMOS, XMM-LSS and ECDF-S fields, $\sim 5\times$ larger than previous comparable studies, providing sufficient volume to determine the halo occupation of the most massive galaxies to $z\sim 4$. We measure the angular two-point correlation function for stellar-mass-selected galaxies over $0.25<z<4$ and by combining the three fields, we reduce the cosmic variance by a factor of $\sim2$ compared to the individual fields. The stellar-to-halo mass ratio peaks at a halo mass of $M_{h}\approx10^{12} {\rm M}_{\odot}$ and is a maximum at $z\sim 1$. For the most massive galaxies, the peak halo mass ($M_{h}^{\rm peak}$) increases from $z=1\rightarrow 0$, while the peak stellar mass in such haloes remains approximately constant, indicating that at $z<1$ massive galaxy formation becomes less efficient. We also find a steady increase in the satellite fraction since $z\sim 1$, from which we infer that massive halos continue to grow hierarchically while their central galaxies experience suppressed stellar-mass growth. We suggest this is due to the central massive galaxies having their growth controlled by feedback from active galactic nuclei feedback, likely from low-power stochastic radio emission.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We report the detection of the convective-transition gap along the lower main sequence (MS) of the Galactic globular cluster (GC) 47 Tucanae using deep James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) observations. We analyse the stellar luminosity function (LF) in the vicinity of the gap location using two independent colour-magnitude diagrams (CMDs). In both cases, the LF is characterised by a narrow gap followed by a discontinuous increase in the stellar number counts, and presents an overall morphology qualitatively consistent with that reported in previous studies. By modelling the observed LFs with an empirical likelihood model, we estimate the statistical significance of the observed feature. Additional JWST passbands exhibit similar signatures, providing further evidence for the presence of a discontinuity.

12. JWST observations of 47 Tucanae: I. The low-mass main sequence down to the hydrogen-burning limit

匹配词：JWST, NIRCam, stellar population
作者：M. Scalco, R. Gerasimov, L. R. Bedin, E. Vesperini, M. Griggio, D. Nardiello, J. Anderson
分类：astro-ph.SR, astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-13

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present the first results of a series of studies based on JWST observations of the globular cluster 47 Tucanae (47 Tuc, or NGC 104) obtained with NIRCam. By combining new data with earlier JWST observations, we derive high-precision proper motions (PMs) over a multi-year baseline (of ~3 years) and construct a clean sample of cluster members extending to very faint magnitudes (F444W~24.25 mag, corresponding to ~0.077 solar masses, slightly above the estimated hydrogen-burning limit of 47 Tuc, ~0.074 solar masses) along the main sequence (MS). We investigated the presence of the low-mass MS kink previously identified in 47 Tuc using the new F090W and F444W photometry. No significant signature of the feature is found using these filters, consistent with its proposed physical interpretation as a consequence of the transition in carbon chemistry toward a methane-dominated equilibrium in very low-mass stars. A comparison with theoretical isochrones indicates that the stellar population near the bottom of the MS is likely dominated by oxygen-poor stars, in agreement with previous findings. Using artificial-star tests, we quantify the completeness of our sample and show that the requirement of PM-based membership significantly limits the detection of the faintest objects, indicating that current JWST observations of 47 Tuc are not sufficiently deep to reliably trace the cluster's brown dwarf cooling sequence. The mass function (MF) of 47 Tuc shows a structured behaviour, with a break at ~0.15 solar masses not associated with the onset of convection, and a mild but systematic radial variation consistent with mass segregation. Our analysis indicates that the MF shape in this mass regime is more complex than a simple power-law description. This feature in the MF may point to a presently unrecognised mechanism affecting the formation or dynamical evolution of low-mass stars in globular clusters.

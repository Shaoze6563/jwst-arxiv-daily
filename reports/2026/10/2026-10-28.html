1. What shapes the bright end of the UV luminosity function? Stellar mass, star-formation variability, and dust in JADES

相关度 13

匹配词：JADES, JWST, NIRCam, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies, star formation, stellar mass, dust attenuation
作者：Amanda Stoffers, Sandro Tacchella, Benjamin D. Johnson, Brant Robertson, Roberto Maiolino, Andrew J. Bunker, Emma Curtis-Lake, A. Lola Danhaive, Qiao Duan, Francesco D'Eugenio
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-27

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

JWST surveys find more UV-bright galaxies at $z>10$ than galaxy-formation models predict, but the roles of recent star formation, stellar mass and dust remain uncertain. We analyse 1365 spectroscopically confirmed JADES galaxies at $3\leq z\leq9$, fitting NIRCam photometry and NIRSpec emission lines with \ceri. Our model includes flexible star-formation histories (SFHs) under three priors, two-component dust attenuation and nebular emission. We account for spectroscopic selection through population reweighting and assess the reliability of inferred trends. At $z=3$--5, rising SFHs are more common in low-mass galaxies, whereas most galaxies above $10^{9.5}\,{\rm M}_{\odot}$ have declining SFHs. The apparent increase in rising fractions with redshift cannot be distinguished from the adopted SFH prior alone. At fixed stellar mass, recent star formation is the strongest predictor of UV brightness. UV-brighter galaxies have higher recent SFRs, younger stellar populations and less attenuation at fixed SFR. Below $10^9\,{\rm M}_{\odot}$, they are also more likely to have rising SFHs, with odds increasing by up to a factor of 2.3 per magnitude brighter, although the strongest trend requires caution. In contrast, galaxies with $M_{\mathrm{UV}}<-20$ at $z=3$--5 are predominantly massive, and approximately half have declining SFHs. Dust partly offsets burst-driven UV brightening: attenuation increases with recent SFR and declines gradually after the inferred SFR peak. In model sensitivity tests, dust has the largest effect on bright-end counts, whereas removing galaxy-to-galaxy SFH scatter changes them by at most 0.17~dex. Thus, bursts can make low-mass galaxies unusually UV-bright for their mass, while the absolute bright end of the UVLF is primarily shaped by stellar mass and dust attenuation.

2. The Role of Star Formation and Morphology in the Mass-Metallicity Relation of Galaxies at Cosmic Noon

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We investigate the dependence of the mass-metallicity relation (MZR) on star formation and galaxy morphology at 1.7<z<3.5, using JWST/NIRISS wide-field slitless spectroscopy of >1000 galaxies. The observations span >85 arcmin^2 from the pure-parallel PASSAGE and OutThere surveys. We derive gas-phase metallicities using a Bayesian framework that combines multiple strong emission-line ratios calibrated against direct auroral-line metallicity measurements from JWST. We constrain stellar masses, star formation rates (SFRs), and morphological properties using ancillary HST and JWST imaging. We stack spectra by stellar mass to constrain the MZR over log(M*/M)~6.5-11, and find that it is well described by a second-order polynomial, with a steep low-mass slope and metallicities<10% solar at log(M*/M)<7.5. Additional stacking by SFR and morphology reveals strong evidence for a fundamental metallicity relation (FMR). Specifically, at log(M*/M)<9.5, galaxies with lower SFRs are more metal-rich than those with higher SFRs at fixed mass. At log(M*/M)<9.5, we find that larger galaxies, and those having lower SFR surface densities, bulges, and/or spiral features are significantly metal rich relative to their smaller, higher SFR surface density, bulge-less, and/or non-spiral counterparts. These results suggest the secondary dependencies of the MZR reflect variations in the baryon cycle, such that recent metal-poor gas accretion drives concentrated star formation and metallicity dilution, while structurally developed galaxies retain more chemically enriched gas reservoirs.

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

To constrain the emergence of the first quiescent galaxies (QGs) and their connection to large-scale structure, we search for rare, high-redshift QGs in proto-clusters using spectral energy distribution modeling, multiple rest-frame color diagnostics, and visual inspection. Our sample includes 12 QG candidates with stellar masses log(M*/Msun) ~ 9-11 in four proto-clusters at z ~ 4-6 within GOODS-North (GOODS-N) and GOODS-South (GOODS-S). The selection uses 11 NIRCam filters in GOODS-N and 17 NIRCam plus 2 MIRI filters in GOODS-S, combined with 9 ancillary HST filters, covering 0.4-7.7 microns. We measure an increasing quiescent fraction with stellar mass, with averages of 1.0%(+0.7/-0.4; 5/502), 3.5%(+2.8/-1.7; 4/115), and 33.3%(+22.2/-17.5; 3/9) for log(M*/Msun) = 9 - 9.75, 9.75 - 10.5, 10.5 - 11.25, respectively, across four proto-clusters. Notably, the two GOODS-N proto-clusters at z=4.406 and z=5.194 have a quiescent fraction of 100% (2/2 at z=4.406; 1/1 at z=5.194) in their highest mass bin (log(M*/Msun) > 10.5), whereas the proto-clusters in GOODS-S at similar redshifts have zero massive quiescent galaxies. Contrary to the expectation that massive galaxies occupy cluster centers at z~0, our QGs are found primarily on the outskirts of overdensity peaks. We also find fewer robust QGs at z>5 (1/8) and none in the z=5.384 proto-cluster, consistent with simulations suggesting that efficient quenching mechanisms had not yet matured. Our results highlight the pronounced variations in quiescent fractions across proto-clusters at z>4. The emerging diversity of proto-cluster QG populations calls for a stronger understanding of the role of environment in early galaxy evolution.

The study of galaxies outside the Milky Way using observations of resolved stellar populations has experienced extraordinary growth over the past two decades. There now exist panchromatic photometry and precise astrometry for hundreds of millions of individually resolved stars in hundreds of galaxies that span $\sim10^9$ in luminosity, $\sim10^3$ in metallicity, and sample a wide range of environments. Here, we review advances in extragalactic astronomy enabled by resolved stellar population imaging. We summarize major observational programs and focus on four interconnected science areas: the star formation histories of galaxies, 'bursty' star formation, the stellar initial mass function, and the orbital histories of Local Group galaxies. We review the literature in each area, synthesize key findings, identify emerging trends, and discuss how new and future facilities (e.g., JWST, LSST, Euclid, Roman, CSST, ELTs, HWO) will enable further progress. Finally, we highlight shortcomings in current datasets and discuss promising directions for new areas of investigation.

5. Does the Escape Fraction Prescription Shape Reionization History and Morphology?

相关度 8

匹配词：JWST, reionization, morphology
作者：Pulkit Sheoran, Ankita Bera, Sultan Hassan
分类：astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-26

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The escape fraction of ionizing photons, $f_{\rm esc}$, is one of the least constrained quantities of the Epoch of Reionization (EoR), and the opacity of the intergalactic medium prevents its direct measurement at high redshifts. We constrain three power-law parameterizations, $f_{\rm esc}(z)$, $f_{\rm esc}(M_{\rm h})$, and $f_{\rm esc}(z, M_{\rm h})$, against JWST UV luminosity functions and EoR observables (the ionizing emissivity, the neutral hydrogen fraction, and the Thomson optical depth) using Markov Chain Monte Carlo, with priors that allow $f_{\rm esc}$ to either increase or decrease with redshift and halo mass. We then propagate the best-fit models into the semi-numerical simulation SimFast21 and compare their ionization morphology and 21 cm power spectra with those of a constant-$f_{\rm esc}$ model. All models reproduce the observations with nearly identical global reionization histories. The data favor an escape fraction that decreases with increasing redshift, from $\approx 0.8$ at $z = 5$ to $\approx 0.35$ at $z = 15$. The halo-mass-only model prefers $f_{\rm esc}$ increasing with halo mass, but this trend acts as a proxy for redshift evolution. When we vary both dependencies jointly, the data prefer a mass-independent $f_{\rm esc}$. At matched global neutral fraction, the ionization morphology is closely similar for all four prescriptions, and the 21 cm power spectra agree to within $\approx 10\%$. These results suggest that, once calibrated against the same observations, the details of the $f_{\rm esc}$ prescription, which are set by the physics of the interstellar medium, largely average out in both the global observables and the 21 cm power spectrum. Distinguishing them may require statistics beyond the power spectrum, such as sightline statistics of the Ly$α$ and 21 cm forests, or forecasts that exploit their small differences in reionization timing.

6. Excitation and Destruction of Small Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons by the Luminous Seyfert Nucleus of NGC 4151

作者：Grant P. Donnelly, J. -D. T. Smith, Sebastian F. Hönig, Daniel A. Dale, Tanio Díaz-Santos, B. T. Draine, Brandon S. Hensley, Thomas S. -Y. Lai, Karin M. Sandstrom, Andrey Vayner
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-27

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present a spatially-resolved detection of $3.3\,\mathrm{μm}$ emission from polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) within the circumnuclear ${\sim}200\,\mathrm{pc}$ of the archetypal Seyfert galaxy NGC 4151, using new JWST NIRSpec/IFU spectroscopy. PAHs are detected down to the innermost $50\,\mathrm{pc}$, though it is likely that PAHs also exist at smaller radii, as PAH detections closer than this seem to be limited by continuum dilution. This is contrary to expectations that PAH grains, especially the smallest grains that carry the $3.3\,\mathrm{μm}$ feature, could not survive near an active galactic nucleus (AGN). Furthermore, the surface brightness of PAH emission increases as radius from the nucleus decreases. We interpret this as evidence that the AGN supplies energy for PAH excitation by driving ultraviolet emission in nearby H and $\mathrm{H_2}$ gas. We do not detect PAH emission within the narrow line region (NLR) that hosts highly ionized and outflowing gas, suggesting that the AGN may also destroy PAHs in these regions, depending on geometry. Together, these results show that an AGN has a complex, multifaceted role in the life cycle of PAHs and in PAH excitation.

作者：Sen-Di Bo, Xing Lu, Tian-Yu Tu, Weiyuan Zhang, Samuel Crowe, Junhao Liu, Shanghuo Li, John Bally
分类：astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.SR
首次提交：2026-10-27

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The Central Molecular Zone (CMZ) at the center of the Galaxy provides a nearby laboratory for studying star formation under extreme physical and chemical conditions. Recent ALMA (Atacama Large Millimeter/Submillimeter Array) observations have identified 43 candidate protostellar outflows in three CMZ clouds. One of these, the Sgr C-F#2 outflow in Sagittarius C (Sgr C), has a highly collimated lobe extending approximately 0.8 pc in projection. Here we present a joint analysis of ALMA observations of SiO J=8-7, 5-4, and 2-1. We use local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE) and non-LTE methods to constrain the SiO column densities, H2 number density, and gas temperature. The low SiO rotational temperatures and higher kinetic temperatures inferred from non-LTE methods suggest subthermal excitation. The elongated SiO morphology, shock-excited gas, position-velocity structure, and the alignment of the red shifted molecular lobe with JWST infrared knots altogether favor jet-dominated entrainment, whereas a broader disk-wind component close to the source cannot be excluded. The outflow shows asymmetries in its morphology and physical properties, which can be attributed to dense gas on the path of its lobe impeding its propagation and feedback from a nearby HII region imposing different pressure on its two sides.

## 1. Can Blue UV-Bright Galaxies Drive Reionization at Cosmic Dawn? An $M_{\rm UV}=-19.6$ Galaxy with $f_{\rm esc}\approx0.85$ at $z=9.244$

- **相关度：** 13
- **匹配词：** JWST, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies, reionization, cosmic dawn, emission line, metallicity
- **作者：** Yunjing Wu, Sijia Cai, Yongda Zhu, Masami Ouchi, Yuichi Harikane, Zheng Cai, Mengtao Tang, Khee-Gan Lee, John D. - **首次提交：** 2026-10-16
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24092) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24092v1)

We revisit EBG-1, a gravitationally lensed galaxy at $z = 9.244$, using newly obtained deep (14 hr) JWST/NIRSpec G235M ($R \sim 1000$) spectroscopy. EBG-1 is one of the bluest galaxies observed at $z > 9$. Along with its remarkably blue continuum of $β= -2.99 \pm 0.15$, we find very weak nebular emission from EBG-1. We obtain a $3σ$ upper limit of EW $< 8.4$ Angstrom for C III], a marginal H$β$ measurement (EW $= 256.2 \pm 122.2$ Angstrom), and a $5σ$ detection of [O III] $λ5007$ (EW $= 882.8 \pm 180.6$ Angstrom). These lines suggest an oxygen abundance of $12 + \log(\mathrm{O/H}) = 7.16 \pm 0.25$ ($\simeq 3\% Z_{\odot}$) and $\log(\mathrm{C/O}) < -0.88$ ($3σ$), placing EBG-1 on the C/O-metallicity sequence traced by lower-redshift galaxies. To constrain the escape fraction ($f_{\rm esc}$), we compare the observed UV slope and emission-line equivalent widths with synthetic spectra generated using BEAGLE. The models favor $f_{\rm esc} \gtrsim 0.75$, with $f_{\rm esc} \approx 0.85$ providing the best overall agreement with the observations. These results suggest that relatively UV-bright galaxies like EBG-1 can achieve the exceptionally high ionizing efficiencies supported by late reionization scenarios.

## 2. Consistently Reproducing Early Galaxy Observations and 21-cm Signal during the Epoch of Reionization

- **相关度：** 11
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxy, galaxies, reionization, high redshift, star formation, morphology
- **作者：** Michele Bianco, Sambit K. Giri, Arghyadeep Basu\orcidlink, Tommaso Boschi, Aurel Schneider, Enrico Garaldi, Garrelt Mellema
- **分类：** astro-ph.CO
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-16
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24064) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24064v1)

In the standard cosmological model, galaxies forming within dark matter haloes are the primary drivers of the Epoch of Reionization (EoR). The complex physics of galaxy formation introduces scatter in the relation between halo mass and star-formation activity, with implications for the production and escape of ionising photons. In this work, we implement a stochastic source model in our cosmological radiative transfer simulations. We calibrate our model against the high-redshift ultraviolet luminosity function observed by JWST and observational constraints on Lyman-continuum leakage to constrain the ionising escape fraction. We find that stochastic star formation redistributes ionising emissivity toward lower-mass haloes, producing a more fragmented ionisation topology and delaying the growth of the largest ionised regions relative to the deterministic, observationally calibrated model. However, when the two models match total ionising photon production, their global neutral fractions and 21-cm power spectrum remain nearly indistinguishable, despite small differences in the morphology of individual ionised regions. Additionally, we establish a unified framework that lets us apply source models derived from computationally expensive hydrodynamical simulations (SPICE, FIRE-2 and Thesan) and investigate their impact on large-volume reionisation simulations, extending their prescriptions to scales of >100 Mpc. We find that these prescriptions produce distinct ionisation topologies and 21-cm statistical signatures, even when their global reionisation histories are similar. Our results highlight the importance of combining physically motivated galaxy formation prescriptions from hydrodynamical simulations with large-volume radiative transfer simulations to interpret forthcoming 21-cm observations and constrain the sources of cosmic reionisation.

## 3. How bright can a galaxy be? Extreme value predictions for high-redshift survey observables

- **相关度：** 11
- **匹配词：** COSMOS, COSMOS-Web, galaxy, galaxies, high redshift, star formation, stellar population, metallicity
- **作者：** Christopher C. Lovell, Theodore Martin, Sandro Tacchella, Christian K. Jespersen, Ian Harrison
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-16
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23016) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23016v1)

Assessing the significance of extreme galaxy candidates has typically relied on redshifts and stellar / halo masses inferred through inverse modelling, which carries substantial systematic uncertainty. We combine the halo mass function with a conditional model for galaxy emission and apply the Extreme Value Statistics (EVS) formalism to predict the distribution of the observed flux of the brightest galaxy expected in a given survey area, redshift interval and photometric band. We find the choice of parametric star formation history (SFH) dominates the modelling uncertainty, introducing a spread approaching $\sim 1 \, \mathrm{dex}$ by $z \sim 18$, whilst the stellar population synthesis model, initial mass function and metallicity each contribute $\lesssim 0.3 \, \mathrm{dex}$. The luminous $z \gtrsim 9$ candidates in COSMOS-Web cluster about the median prediction for the area they were drawn from, and lie at least an order of magnitude below the $3σ$ limit, a margin comparable to the SFH systematic. Of the observational samples reaching beyond $z \sim 13$, the two spectroscopically confirmed galaxies at $z \simeq 14$ lie close to the median prediction for their area, whereas photometric candidates at $z \gtrsim 16$ reach $+4σ$ (a probability to exceed of $\sim 10^{-5}$), the brightest lying at the limit obtained by converting all halo baryons into stars. Since the prediction bounds the flux far more tightly than the photometry bounds the redshift, such offsets are most naturally resolved by errors in the inferred redshifts. This approach allows extreme candidates to be assessed directly in the observational plane, making explicit the modelling assumptions common to forward and inverse approaches.

We present a near-infrared spectroscopic follow-up study of seven H$α$ emitter candidates at $z\sim2$ in the ZFOURGE-COSMOS field, selected via the $K_{\rm s}$-band flux excess method. Spectra obtained with Subaru/SWIMS confirm strong H$α$ emission line at $z=2.06-2.49$ for all the targets. Three galaxies are low-mass systems ($\log(M_\star/M_\odot) < 9$) with high specific SFRs, while four are high-mass systems ($\log(M_\star/M_\odot) > 10$) on the star-forming main sequence. The low-mass galaxies show consistent H$α$ flux measurements between the $K_{\rm s}$-band flux excess method and the spectroscopy, whereas the high-mass galaxies show higher H$α$ flux inferred from the $K_{\rm s}$-band flux excess than the spectroscopic measurement which may be attributed to their extended star formation distributions. The low-mass galaxies exhibit extreme [O III] $\lambda5007$ emission with high rest-frame equivalent widths ($\mathrm{EW}_{\mathrm{rest}}$) ranging from 480 to 1064 $\mathring{\mathrm{A}}$. They are characterized by high [O III]/H$β$ ratios ($>4$) and weak [O II] and [N II] emission lines, indicating high ionization parameters and low metallicity. The line ratios are comparable to those of star-forming galaxies at $z \gtrsim 6$ and their local analogs. JWST/NIRCam imaging reveals a clear mass-dependent color gradient: the low-mass galaxies present positive F115W-F277W radial gradients with blue cores, while the high-mass galaxies exhibit negative gradients with red cores. These results suggest distinct assembly pathways for low- and high-mass galaxies during the cosmic noon epoch.

## 6. Dust in Galaxies at $z=10-15$: Attenuation and Re-emission Predictions from the FirstLight Simulations

- **相关度：** 9
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxies, stellar mass, dust attenuation, metallicity
- **作者：** Yurina Nakazato, Akio K. Inoue, Rachel S. Somerville, Daisuke Toyouchi, Kosei Matsumoto, Daniel Ceverino
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-16
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22766) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.22766v1)

Dust masses and grain properties in $z>10$ galaxies remain poorly constrained, as JWST UV attenuation and ALMA dust re-emission are rarely modelled consistently. We perform post-processed 3D dust radiative transfer on 208 snapshots of 99 galaxies at $z=10$-15 from the FirstLight simulations ($M_*=10^{6.5}$-$10^{9.5}\,M_\odot$), applying twelve dust models per snapshot (2496 runs): four dust-to-metal (DTM) prescriptions and three grain size distributions. Metallicity-dependent DTM models reproduce ALMA dust-mass upper limits at $z\gtrsim10$ and detections at $z\sim7$, as dust-to-stellar mass ratios rise sharply near $Z\sim0.2\,Z_\odot$, typically at $M_*\gtrsim10^{8.5}\,M_\odot$, where grain growth by metal accretion becomes dominant. A constant DTM of 0.4 instead violates more than half of the upper limits. For dust attenuation, SMC-like dust is favored by the observed $M_{\rm UV}$-$β_{\rm UV}$ relation. The observed UV slopes ($β_{\rm UV}\simeq-2.3$) favor small grains ($a\lesssimλ_{\rm UV}/2π\simeq0.025\,μ{\rm m}$) producing steep UV extinction, together with a low abundance of carbonaceous grains or PAHs responsible for the 2175 $\mathrm{\mathring{A}}$ bump, consistent with silicate-rich dust. Large-grain stellar dust ($a_0=0.1\,μ{\rm m}$) instead yields nearly grey extinction and UV slopes close to the intrinsic value ($β_{\rm UV,0}\simeq-2.6$). SMC and MW attenuation curves flatten at $A_{\rm V}\gtrsim0.3$, showing that relatively flat attenuation does not necessarily imply large grains. For dust re-emission, dust peak temperatures reach 60-80 K, exceeding the 40-50 K commonly assumed in ALMA dust-mass estimates. Predicted IR continuum flux increases with UV luminosity; at current ALMA sensitivities (10-20 $μ$Jy), detections generally require $M_{\rm UV}\lesssim-21.5$. Roman wide-field surveys may identify such rare UV-bright galaxies.

The connection between galaxy structure, star formation activity, and large-scale environments provides important insights into the evolution of massive galaxies. However, the relative importance of different galaxy properties in predicting quenching and the environmental dependence of structurally distinct galaxy populations remain poorly constrained at cosmic noon ($1\leq z<3$). In this work, we investigate massive galaxies with $\log M_*/{\rm M}_{\odot}>10.5$ at $1\leq z<3$ using deep JWST/NIRCam imaging from the COSMOS-Web survey, combining measurements of central stellar mass surface density ($Σ_1$), star formation activity, and galaxy clustering. We find that $Σ_1$ is the most informative predictor of quenching among the considered properties, including stellar mass, effective radius, formation redshift, and local overdensity. The quenched fraction shows a strong dependence on $Σ_1$, increasing rapidly above a characteristic $Σ_1$. These results highlight the importance of $Σ_1$ in tracing quenching and reveal that structural diversity among star-forming galaxies is not strongly reflected in their large-scale environments, providing new constraints on the interplay between galaxy structure, quenching, and halo environments during cosmic noon.

## 8. Beyond the Disc--Spheroid Dichotomy at $0.6 &lt; z &lt; 8.0$: Evidence for a Morphological Indeterminacy Regime from JWST

- **相关度：** 8
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxy, galaxies, stellar mass, morphology
- **作者：** Igor Kolesnikov, Reinaldo Ramos de Carvalho, Jeong Hwan Lee, Rodrigo Flores-Freitas, Vitor M. Sampaio, Luiz A. Silva-Lima
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-16
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24078) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24078v1)

Galaxy morphology is a fundamental tracer of galaxy assembly, but its characterization becomes increasingly uncertain in the early Universe. We investigate morphological classifications across different methodologies over the redshift range $0.6 < z < 8.0$, using JWST observations of 15,567 galaxies with 6,099 synthetic galaxies from IllustrisTNG. To quantify morphology without relying on visual labels or prescribed light profiles, we use an unsupervised-supervised hybrid machine-learning approach based on the non-parametric MEGG system, which combines the second moment of the light distribution, Entropy, Gini, and Gradient Pattern Analysis. We find that parametric and non-parametric classifications remain broadly consistent at $z < 2.5$, but diverge systematically at higher redshift. We identify this epoch as the Morphological Indeterminacy Regime (MIR), in which classical disc and spheroidal categories no longer provide a unique description of the galaxy population. The fraction of mixed-interpretation galaxies increases with redshift, reaching $F_{\rm MIR} \sim 0.55$ at $z > 2.5$. Cross-domain and projection-invariance tests show that the classifications are stable and insensitive to data set and viewing angle. In the interval $2.5 < z < 8.0$, MIR galaxies occupy an intermediate stellar-mass regime (median; $\log(M_\star/M_\odot) \sim 9.7$), between low-mass discs (median; $\log(M_\star/M_\odot) \sim 9.5$) and massive spheroids (median; $\log(M_\star/M_\odot) \sim 10.1$), while irregular systems have stellar-mass distributions similar to those of MIR galaxies. We interpret these results as evidence that morphological indeterminacy is a characteristic feature of galaxy assembly in the early Universe, reflecting a population of dynamically young systems that have not yet settled into the classical Hubble sequence.

## 10. Mid-infrared Imaging of SR 12 c with JWST/MIRI: Grain Growth in an Evolved Circumplanetary Disk

- **匹配词：** JWST, MIRI, spectral energy distribution
- **作者：** Ya-Lin Wu, Claire O. Finley, Brendan P. Bowler, Ning Chen, Yuhiko Aoyama, Gregory J. Herczeg, Catherine C. Espaillat, William Best, Ian Czekala, Katherine B. Follette, Adam L. Kraus, Raquel A. Martinez, Connor E. Robinson, Quang H. Tran, Kimberly Ward-Duong, Yifan Zhou

- **首次提交：** 2026-10-17
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24776) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24776v1)

The physical properties of circumplanetary disks offer insights into the formation of giant planets and their satellites. The circumbinary planetary-mass companion SR 12 c (1200 au from SR 12 AB) hosts an isolated dust disk directly detected with ALMA. We present JWST/MIRI imaging of SR 12 c from 5.6 to 21 $μ$m and analyze its spectral energy distribution from infrared to submillimeter wavelengths. Phenomenologically, the dust disk can be described by a two-component blackbody with large contrasts in characteristic temperatures and radii. Physically motivated models with varied geometries and heating mechanisms suggest a predominantly flat, irradiation-heated disk with a smaller contribution from viscous dissipation. The disk is inferred to be compact, with an inner radius of ~3 $R_{\rm Jup}$. The outer radius is only loosely constrained, with a characteristic value of ~3 au. The lack of strong 10 $μ$m excess emission suggests significant grain growth and dust settling. We also find that SR 12 c is broadly consistent with the empirical star-disk scaling relations, providing context for its possible formation pathway. These results demonstrate the power of combining mid-infrared and submillimeter observations with disk modeling to better understand the physical properties of circumplanetary disks.

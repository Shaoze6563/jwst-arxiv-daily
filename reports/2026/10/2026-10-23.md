## 1. Self-Supervised Representation Learning for JWST Galaxy Images and Low Resolution Spectra

- **相关度：** 10
- **匹配词：** JWST, galaxy, stellar mass, SED fitting
- **作者：** Yang Cheng, Morgan Fouesneau, Ivelina G. Momcheva, Tobias Buck, Shengxiu Sun
- **分类：** astro-ph.GA
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-22
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31078) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31078v1)

A self-supervised multimodal model recovers galaxy stellar masses from sparse JWST data that lack the multi-band coverage required by traditional SED fitting. We train on single-band F150W imaging paired with observed-frame 1-2 $μ$m PRISM spectra and obtain a joint image-spectrum representation that recovers stellar mass at $1<z\le3$ with $R^2 \sim 0.9$ and $σ_{\mathrm{NMAD}} = 0.24$ dex; cross-modal CLIP alignment further raises single-modality performance by $ΔR^2 = +0.04$ (imaging) and $+0.09$ (spectroscopy), narrowing the gap to joint-modality inference. The code and documentation are available at \href{https://github.com/2003cy/neurips2026_ssl_jwst}{GitHub}.

## 2. A cosmic filament catalog for COSMOS-Web Data Release 1 across 0.5 &lt; z &lt; 3.5

- **相关度：** 6
- **匹配词：** COSMOS, COSMOS-Web, galaxy, galaxies
- **作者：** Qiaoyang Hao, Tao Wang, Weishan Zhu, Hong Guo, Peng Wang, Tiancheng Yang, Luwenjia Zhou
- **分类：** astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.IM
- **首次提交：** 2026-10-22
- **链接：** [摘要](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31168) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31168v1)

Cosmic filaments represent the environments in which most galaxies form and evolve. However, the spatial distribution of filaments and the link between galaxy properties and their positions relative to filaments remains unclear, particularly at high redshifts. In this work, using the deep-field catalog COSMOS-Web Data Release 1, we construct a filament catalog spanning 0.5 < z < 3.5 that can be directly applied to studies of galaxy evolution and the galaxy--large-scale-structure connection at the peak epoch of galaxy and black hole formation. We developed a new procedure based on the DisPerSE method that fully incorporates the probability distribution functions (PDFs) of galaxy photometric redshifts by generating multiple realizations for each tomographic 2D slice. We then stacked these realizations to construct ``filament probability maps'' (w_ fil maps), from which filaments were subsequently extracted. The filament extraction parameters were optimized using mock catalogs generated from TNG simulations by minimizing the discrepancies between 3D and projected 2D filaments. Applying this procedure to the COSMOS-Web data, we detected 12,523 filaments (comprising 136,210 segments) across 0.5 < z < 3.5 and released a filament catalog, a companion segment catalog, and filament probability maps for each slice. Validation against astrophysical properties shows a strong agreement between the extracted filaments and those in the simulations, supporting the robustness of the filament extraction. These data products provide a robust foundation for investigating galaxy--environment coevolution at cosmic noon and beyond.

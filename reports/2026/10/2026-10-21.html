1. The clumpy fraction of galaxies at $z=1-9$ with JADES: evidence for a mass-dependent transition in clump origin

相关度 10

匹配词：JADES, JWST, NIRCam, galaxies, high redshift, stellar mass
作者：Dávid Puskás, Sandro Tacchella, Phoebe Chapman, Floor van Donkelaar, Andrew J. Bunker, Courtney Carreira, A. Lola Danhaive, Francesco D'Eugenio, Qiao Duan, Patricia Iglesias-Navarro, Amanda Stoffers, Hannah Übler, Yongda Zhu, Mirko Curti, Emma Curtis-Lake, Kevin Hainline, Eiichi Egami, Daniel J. Eisenstein, Zhiyuan Ji, Benjamin D. Johnson, Roberto Maiolino, Marcia Rieke, Brant Robertson, Christina C. Williams
分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-20

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We measure the fraction of clumpy galaxies ($f_{\rm clumpy}$) over the redshift range $z=1-9$ and stellar masses $\log_{10}(M_\star/{\rm M_\odot}) \geq 8$ using JWST/NIRCam imaging from JADES. We define clumps as compact off-centre surface-brightness peaks with characteristic sizes of a few hundred parsecs, and develop a dendrogram-based clump-finding method with astrodendro to identify them directly in rest-frame UV, blue, and optical images. Clump-detection completeness is quantified through injection-recovery simulations, which we use to define ($M_\star$, $z$)-dependent 50 per cent completeness-limited $f_{\rm clumpy}$, alongside a probability-weighted completeness-corrected statistic for comparison. For the full sample, the blue and optical $f_{\rm clumpy}$ reach $\approx55$ per cent at $z\approx1-2$ and decline to $\lesssim5$ per cent by $z\approx8-9$, while the UV fraction remains higher at $\approx12$ per cent. At $z\approx1-2$, $f_{\rm clumpy}$ increases strongly with stellar mass, from $\approx35$ per cent in the lowest-mass bin to $\approx85$ per cent at $\log_{10}(M_\star/{\rm M_\odot})\geq10$. Most clumpy galaxies host only one or two robustly detected off-centre clumps, while higher-multiplicity systems occur preferentially in massive, strongly star-forming galaxies at low to intermediate redshift. Comparison with major merger rates suggests that mergers could account for a substantial fraction of low-mass ($\log_{10}(M_\star/{\rm M_\odot})<8.5$), high-redshift clumpy galaxies, while the observed fractions in more massive systems ($\log_{10}(M_\star/{\rm M_\odot})>9$) require an additional in situ clump-formation channel. Our results are consistent with a transition from merger-dominated clumpy morphologies at early times and low stellar masses to an increasing contribution from in situ clump-formation channels, such as disc instabilities, in more massive systems.

2. The Future of Gamma-Ray Bursts as Probes of the Epoch of Reionization in the Era of JWST and the Extremely Large Telescope

作者：H. M. Fausey, A. J. van der Horst, N. R. Tanvir, K. Wiersema
分类：astro-ph.CO
首次提交：2026-10-19

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

The Epoch of Reionization (EoR) is a key era of cosmological history that remains poorly understood. An examination of the Ly-alpha damping wing of high-redshift GRBs can provide direct measurements of the fraction of neutral hydrogen (i.e., neutral fraction) of the IGM in the vicinity of the GRB host galaxy, in addition to the neutral column in the host along the sight-line to the GRB. The latter quantity can help infer the fraction of ionizing photons escaping the galaxy (i.e. escape fraction); the average escape fraction being a key input to EoR models. New and upcoming observatories such as the JWST and the Extremely Large Telescope (ELT) can obtain more robust estimates of the hydrogen column density and neutral fraction around high-redshift GRBs. We simulate spectra for the JWST NIRSpec and ELT HARMONI spectrographs using different GRB magnitudes and exposure times to determine their capabilities for measuring column densities and neutral fractions with the Ly-alpha damping wings of high-redshift GRBs. We find that both spectrographs will be highly effective for obtaining column density estimates. Despite the presence of telluric lines, we find that ELT HARMONI will be a powerful instrument for retrieving accurate neutral fraction estimates, but JWST NIRSpec will be vital for obtaining neutral fraction measurements for bright GRBs that fall in redshift ranges where ELT is less effective. Additionally, shorter JWST Target of Opportunity (ToO) follow-up times would greatly increase the accuracy of neutral fraction estimates obtained with NIRSpec spectra.

3. Leviathan: a very massive, extended sub-millimetre galaxy spectroscopically confirmed at $\rm z = 4.05$

相关度 9

匹配词：JWST, NIRSpec, galaxy, galaxies, star formation, stellar mass, SED fitting, quiescent
作者：William M. Baker, Francesco Valentino, Jens Hjorth, Gabriel Brammer, Ugnė Dudzevičiūtė, Kei Ito, Antonio Pensabene, Pengpei Zhu
分类：astro-ph.GA, astro-ph.CO
首次提交：2026-10-19

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

We present Leviathan, a sub-millimetre galaxy (SMG) spectroscopically confirmed at $z=4.051$ with JWST/NIRSpec. Modelling 31 bands of photometry from the rest-frame ultraviolet to the millimetre, we find a stellar mass of $\log(M_\star/M_\odot)=11.34^{+0.15}_{-0.22}$, making Leviathan one of the most massive spectroscopically confirmed galaxies at $z>4$, yet consistent with $Λ$CDM for a star-formation efficiency of approximately 20%. Although SMGs are often thought to be building the dense cores of compact quiescent galaxies, Leviathan is extended, with a rest-frame $0.9\,μ\mathrm{m}$ half-light radius of 4.3 kpc. Spatially resolved SED fitting gives a half-mass radius of $3.1\pm0.4$ kpc, consistent with its size in rest-frame $1.5\,μ\mathrm{m}$ imaging. This is three times larger than quiescent galaxies of the same mass, but consistent with the size-mass relation of star-forming galaxies at $4<z<5$ extrapolated to $M_\star=10^{11.3}M_\odot$. Leviathan forms stars on the main sequence (approximately $400\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}$) with dust spread across its disc. Both its stellar mass and dust are more extended than those of $z\approx3$ SMGs. Alongside GN20.2b, which has a similar mass and star-formation rate but is three times smaller, these show that massive dusty galaxies at $z\approx4$ can have very different stellar structures, and that not all have their stellar mass concentrated in a compact core.

4. Inferring the shapes of low-mass galaxies at $z>6$ from FIREbox$^{HR}$. Where are the disks?

分类：astro-ph.GA
首次提交：2026-10-18

arXiv 摘要 · PDF

Context. The three-dimensional shapes of galaxies provide clues to their formation and evolution. Inferring intrinsic shapes from projected sizes and axis ratios requires assumptions about the population distribution of shape parameters. Aims. We test commonly adopted assumptions in Bayesian shape inference for high-redshift galaxies. Methods. We analyze FIREboxHR galaxies with $\log(M_\star/M_\odot)=6.5-9.5$ at $z\sim6-15$. We measure intrinsic shapes from stellar particles using the minimum covariance determinant (MCD) estimator. We also create mock JWST/NIRCam images, measure half-light radii and axis ratios, and infer intrinsic distributions from the joint distribution of projected axis ratio $q$ and size $\log a$. Results. Low-mass galaxies are predominantly prolate, with a substantial spheroidal component. Intrinsic ellipticity $E$ and triaxiality $T$ are well described by Johnson SB distributions, while intrinsic size $A$ is approximately log-normal. We therefore model $(\mathrm{logit}(E),\mathrm{logit}(T),\log A)$ with a multivariate normal distribution and allow general correlations. This model recovers the particle-measured shape distributions more faithfully than a multivariate normal model in $(E,T,\log A)$. Triaxiality-related parameters remain difficult to constrain from projected data without large samples or informative priors. Conclusions. Bayesian forward modeling can recover intrinsic shape distributions when its population model adequately describes the underlying galaxies. An unsuitable distribution can bias inferences about morphological evolution at high redshift.
